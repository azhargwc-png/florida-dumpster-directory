## Placement, Swales and Permits in Rotonda West

Rotonda West is in unincorporated Charlotte County, and it is swale country, so placement is the key local issue.

**On your own driveway:** a box entirely on your private driveway usually needs no permit.

**On the street or swale:** Rotonda West's lots almost all front a drainage swale, part of the community's canal-and-swale stormwater system. The swale is Charlotte County right-of-way, not an extension of your driveway, so a box there usually needs a county right-of-way permit, and placing a heavy box on a swale without one can draw a fine and damage the drainage grade. Keep the box on your driveway unless you pull the permit.

**Deed restrictions.** Rotonda West is a deed-restricted community, and the Rotonda West Association may have its own rules on dumpster placement and duration. Check the association's rules before booking, in addition to the county rules.

**Canal-lot access and driveway protection.** On a tight canal lot, confirm the delivery truck's approach, and protect paver or sealed driveways with boards under the contact points.

## Storm and Hurricane Debris in Rotonda West

Rotonda West was within Hurricane Ian's impact zone, and storm debris follows different rules from a normal dumpster job. Use a private dumpster for ineligible material, private-property debris and rebuild work. Keep household hazardous waste out and take it to Charlotte County's Mini-Transfer HHW drop-off.
