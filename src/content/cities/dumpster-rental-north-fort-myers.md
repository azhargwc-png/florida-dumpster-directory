## Placement and Permits in North Fort Myers

North Fort Myers is unincorporated Lee County, so county right-of-way rules apply.

**On your own driveway or lot:** a box entirely on your private property usually needs no permit.

**On the street or right-of-way:** a box on a public right-of-way in unincorporated Lee County usually needs a county right-of-way permit. On lots that front a drainage swale, keep the box on your property unless you pull the permit, since the swale is right-of-way.

**Mobile-home community placement:** in a manufactured-housing or mobile-home community, the park or community management often controls where a box can sit and for how long, and lots can be tight. Clear the placement with management before booking, and a smaller box may be the practical fit.

**Driveway protection:** protect paver and sealed driveways with boards under the contact points.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need a permit for a dumpster in North Fort Myers?**
A dumpster entirely on your own driveway or lot in North Fort Myers usually needs no permit. If it sits on a street, sidewalk, swale or other public right-of-way, unincorporated Lee County usually requires a right-of-way permit. On lots that front a drainage swale, keep the box on your property unless you pull the permit, since the swale is right-of-way. In a mobile-home community, park management also controls placement, so clear it with them first.

**Can I rent a dumpster for a mobile-home cleanout in North Fort Myers?**
Yes. A 15- or 20-yard box usually handles a mobile-home cleanout or light renovation, and a 30-yard may be needed for a full gut or a manufactured-home demolition. Because North Fort Myers has many mobile-home and manufactured-housing communities, local haulers handle this regularly. The main thing to confirm is placement: park or community management often controls where a box can sit and for how long, and lots can be tight, so clear it with management before booking.

**What size dumpster do I need in North Fort Myers?**
It depends on the job. A mobile-home cleanout fits a 15- or 20-yard box, a shingle roof a 20-yard, a whole-home remodel or estate cleanout a 20- or 30-yard, and heavy concrete or masonry a 10-yard filled partway. Tile roofs go in a 10-yard sized by weight. Confirm the size by weight with the hauler, and for a mobile-home community, confirm the box fits the lot and park rules.

**Where does household hazardous waste go in North Fort Myers?**
Household hazardous waste cannot go in a roll-off. In North Fort Myers, paint, motor oil, solvents, pesticides, batteries, propane tanks and electronics go to Lee County's Topaz Court household hazardous waste facility. Florida Statute 403.708 also restricts lead-acid batteries, used oil and large appliances from standard landfill disposal. Separate these items before loading a dumpster, and ask the hauler for their full accepted-items list to avoid a contamination charge.
