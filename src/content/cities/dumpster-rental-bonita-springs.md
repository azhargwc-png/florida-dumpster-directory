## Placement, Permits and HOAs in Bonita Springs

Bonita Springs is an incorporated city in Lee County, so City of Bonita Springs rules apply inside the city, and in many neighborhoods the HOA is the first rule to check.

**On your own driveway:** a box entirely on your private driveway usually needs no government permit.

**On the street or right-of-way:** a box on a public right-of-way typically needs a permit from the city or county that owns the road.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need a permit for a dumpster in Bonita Springs?**
A dumpster entirely on your own driveway in Bonita Springs usually needs no government permit. A box on a street or public right-of-way typically needs a permit from the City of Bonita Springs or Lee County, depending on who owns the road. Just as important here, your homeowner association often has its own rules on dumpster placement and duration, so check the HOA before you book, since an association can require you to move or screen the box.

**Do Bonita Springs HOAs have rules about dumpsters?**
Many do. Bonita Springs has numerous gated golf and waterfront communities whose covenants govern where a dumpster can sit, how long it can stay, and sometimes require advance notice or screening before delivery. These HOA rules often apply on top of, and can be stricter than, the city and county rules. Check with your association before booking, and choose a hauler familiar with how local communities handle dumpster placement.

**What size dumpster do I need in Bonita Springs?**
Most Bonita Springs projects fit a 20-yard box, the standard for remodels, shingle re-roofs and cleanouts. Tile roofs, concrete and masonry go in a 10-yard box filled partway, since dense material hits the weight limit before the box looks full. A full gut may need a 30-yard, and a lanai or pool-cage tear-down fits a 20- or 30-yard since the aluminum is light. Confirm the size by weight with the hauler.

**What size dumpster for a lanai or pool-cage tear-down in Bonita Springs?**
A lanai, pool cage or screen enclosure is mostly aluminum framing and screen, bulky but light, so a 20- or 30-yard box sized by volume usually fits, and weight is rarely the limit. This work is common on Bonita Springs waterfront and golf-community homes and spikes after storms damage enclosures. If the tear-down also includes a concrete deck or footers, keep that heavy material in a separate 10-yard box filled partway.

**Where does household hazardous waste go in Bonita Springs?**
Household hazardous waste cannot go in a roll-off. In Bonita Springs, paint, motor oil, solvents, pesticides, batteries, propane tanks and electronics go to Lee County's Topaz Court household hazardous waste facility. Florida Statute 403.708 also restricts lead-acid batteries, used oil and large appliances from standard landfill disposal. Separate these items before loading a dumpster, and ask the hauler for their full accepted-items list.
