## Placement and Permits in Lehigh Acres

Lehigh Acres is unincorporated Lee County, so county right-of-way rules apply.

**On your own driveway or lot:** a dumpster entirely on your private property usually needs no permit, and Lehigh Acres lots are generally roomy enough to place a box without trouble.

**On the street or right-of-way:** a box on a public right-of-way in unincorporated Lee County usually needs a county right-of-way permit. On the many lots that front a drainage swale, keep the box on your own property unless you pull the permit, since the swale is right-of-way, not driveway.

**New-construction sites:** on a build, the box often sits on the lot itself and needs no right-of-way permit, but confirm whether the general contractor or the owner is responsible for the dumpster and any permit.

**Driveway protection:** protect paver and sealed driveways with boards under the contact points.

## Lee County Construction and Disposal Rules

Because Lehigh Acres produces so much construction debris, the Lee County rules matter here. Covered construction-and-demolition projects in Lee County face a 50% recycling-or-reuse requirement under county ordinance, with a certification form, which affects contractors pulling permits. A hauler who works Lehigh Acres construction regularly will know which approved facilities take the load and how to meet the requirement. Separating clean materials like concrete and wood makes meeting it easier and can lower disposal cost. **What size dumpster do I need for new construction in Lehigh Acres?**
New construction and additions usually fit a 20- or 30-yard box depending on the phase, since the debris is bulky but lighter.
