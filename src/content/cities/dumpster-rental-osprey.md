## Placement, Permits and HOAs in Osprey

Osprey is unincorporated Sarasota County, so county right-of-way rules apply, and in gated communities the HOA is often the first rule to check.

**On your own driveway:** a box entirely on your private driveway usually needs no county permit.

**On the street or right-of-way:** a box on a public right-of-way usually needs a Sarasota County right-of-way permit.

**HOA rules come first in gated Osprey communities.** Gated and waterfront neighborhoods like The Oaks frequently have covenants on where a dumpster can sit, how long it can stay, and sometimes requiring advance notice or screening. ## Storm, Yard Waste and Hazardous Waste in Osprey

Osprey is a coastal Sarasota County community and sees storm impact. Use the Sarasota County curbside program for eligible, separated storm debris when it is running after a declared event, following the county's Code 106-47 disaster-debris separation rules, and a private dumpster for ineligible material, private-property debris and rebuild work, within any HOA rules. Keep yard waste separate through the county's Leaf It Out program, and take household hazardous waste to Sarasota County's Chemical Collection Centers, never a roll-off. Florida Statute 403.708 restricts lead-acid batteries, used oil and large appliances from standard landfill disposal.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need a permit for a dumpster in Osprey?**
A dumpster entirely on your own driveway in Osprey usually needs no county permit. A box on a street or public right-of-way usually needs a Sarasota County right-of-way permit. Just as important in Osprey's gated communities, your homeowner association often has its own rules on dumpster placement and duration, so check the HOA before you book, since an association can require you to move or screen the box regardless of the county rule.

**Do Osprey HOAs have rules about dumpsters?**
Many do. Osprey's gated and waterfront communities, such as The Oaks, frequently have covenants governing where a dumpster can sit, how long it can stay, and sometimes requiring advance notice or screening before delivery. These HOA rules often apply on top of, and can be stricter than, the county rules. Check with your association before booking, and choose a hauler familiar with how Osprey communities handle dumpster placement.
