**Can I put yard waste in a dumpster in Nokomis?**
Sarasota County keeps yard waste separate through its Leaf It Out program, which handles vegetative debris outside the regular stream and excludes lumber, fencing and treated wood as not being yard waste. Florida Statute 403.708 also restricts lead-acid batteries, used oil and large appliances from standard landfill disposal.
