## Florida Treats Yard Waste Differently

Under Florida law, yard trash is handled separately from regular solid waste, and Florida Statute 403.708 restricts yard trash from standard landfill disposal, with limited exceptions. In practice that means vegetative debris is usually routed to mulching, composting or a dedicated yard-waste facility rather than the regular landfill, and haulers often keep it in a separate, clean load. Counties across Southwest Florida run their own yard-waste rules and curbside programs, Sarasota County's "Leaf It Out" program, for example, sets out how vegetative waste is collected and specifically excludes lumber, fencing and treated wood, which are not yard waste. Check your county's program first; for routine volumes it may be free at the curb.

## When to Rent a Yard Waste Dumpster vs Use Curbside

Use the county curbside program when the volume fits its limits, routine trimming, a few bags of clippings, a modest pile of fronds. Rent a dumpster when the job is bigger than curbside will take: a full landscaping overhaul, lot or acreage clearing, large tree removal, or a storm cleanup that overwhelms collection. After a declared hurricane, counties often run a separate storm-debris collection for eligible, correctly-separated vegetative debris at the curb, use that program when it applies, and reserve a private dumpster for ineligible debris, private-property piles crews will not enter, or cleanup that cannot wait for the collection schedule.

## What Is and Isn't Yard Waste

Yard waste is vegetative: palm fronds, tree limbs and branches, leaves, grass clippings, shrubs, root balls and plant material. It is *not* lumber, pressure-treated or painted wood, fencing, construction debris, dirt or rock, those are different categories with different disposal routes, even though they come from the yard. Mixing non-vegetative material into a yard-waste load can contaminate it, defeat the composting or mulching route, and raise the cost. Keep a yard-waste box clean and vegetative.

## Sizing and Weight

Vegetative debris is bulky and relatively light when dry, so yard-waste jobs are usually sized by volume, a [20-yard](/20-yard-dumpster-rental/) box suits most landscaping overhauls, with a [30-yard](/30-yard-dumpster-rental/) for larger clearing. The catch is moisture: wet, green or freshly-cut Florida vegetation is far heavier than dry brush, and a box of soaked fronds and limbs after a storm can approach its weight limit. Tell the hauler whether the debris is green and wet or dry, and ask how that affects the box size and the weight allowance.

## Placement

A yard-waste box follows the same placement rules as any roll-off: own driveway, usually no permit; street, sidewalk or swale, usually a right-of-way permit. On a large clearing job, position the box for easy loading from the work area.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need a dumpster for yard waste in Florida?**
Often not for routine amounts, Florida counties run curbside yard-waste programs that handle regular trimming and modest piles, frequently at no extra cost. Rent a dumpster when the volume exceeds what curbside will take: a landscaping overhaul, lot clearing, large tree removal, or storm cleanup that overwhelms collection. After a hurricane, counties often collect eligible, separated vegetative debris at the curb, so use that program when it applies and reserve a dumpster for the rest.

**What counts as yard waste?**
Yard waste is vegetative material: palm fronds, tree limbs and branches, leaves, grass clippings, shrubs, root balls and plant matter. It does not include lumber, pressure-treated or painted wood, fencing, construction debris, dirt or rock, even though those come from the yard, they follow different disposal routes. Keeping a yard-waste load purely vegetative lets it go to mulching or composting and avoids contamination charges.

**Why is yard waste handled separately from regular trash?**
Florida law treats vegetative debris differently from household solid waste, and Florida Statute 403.708 restricts yard trash from standard landfill disposal with limited exceptions. Vegetative waste is usually routed to mulching, composting or a dedicated facility instead. County programs like Sarasota County's Leaf It Out set specific rules for how it is collected and what counts, which is why haulers keep yard waste in a separate, clean load.

**How heavy is a yard waste dumpster?**
It depends on moisture. Dry brush and fronds are light and bulky, so yard-waste jobs are usually sized by volume. But wet, green or freshly-cut Florida vegetation is much heavier, and a box of soaked limbs and fronds after a storm can approach its weight limit. Tell the hauler whether the debris is green and wet or dry so they can size the box and set the weight allowance correctly.
