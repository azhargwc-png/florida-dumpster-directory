# How to Rent a Dumpster: Step by Step

Renting a dumpster is straightforward once you know the steps. This walks through the whole process, from figuring out the size to the box being hauled away.

## The Steps

**First, size the job.** Decide roughly how much debris you have and whether any of it is heavy (concrete, tile, dirt), since that sets the box size and weight allowance.

**Second, call a local company.** Describe the job, the debris, the rough volume, the days you need, and the delivery location, and they recommend a size and give a quote.

**Third, sort the placement and permit.** Pick a spot (driveway is simplest), and confirm whether a street or swale placement needs a permit.

**Fourth, take delivery.** The truck drops the box on the agreed spot. Protect a paver driveway with boards.

**Fifth, load it.** Keep the load level with the fill line, keep heavy debris within the weight allowance, and keep banned items out.

**Sixth, schedule pickup.** Call when you are done, or at the end of the period, and the truck hauls it away.
