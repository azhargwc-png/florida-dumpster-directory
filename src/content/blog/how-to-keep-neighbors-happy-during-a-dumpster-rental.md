# How to Keep Neighbors Happy During a Dumpster Rental

A dumpster affects your neighbors too, so a little consideration keeps the peace. This post covers being a good neighbor during a rental.

## Being a Considerate Neighbor

To keep neighbors happy during a rental:

- **Keep it on your own property.** A box on your driveway, not blocking a shared area, the street or a neighbor's access.
- **Mind the duration.** Do not leave the box longer than needed; a dumpster sitting for weeks is an eyesore.
- **Keep it tidy.** Load debris in, not around, the box, and keep the area clear of blown debris.
- **Keep the load level.** An overflowing box looks bad and sheds debris.
- **Give a heads-up** for a big or long project, so neighbors know what to expect.
- **Mind noise hours** for delivery and pickup, early-morning truck noise can irritate.
- **Secure loose debris** so wind (common in Florida) does not blow it into yards.

Consideration, right placement, reasonable duration, a tidy area, is what keeps a rental from becoming a neighbor dispute.
