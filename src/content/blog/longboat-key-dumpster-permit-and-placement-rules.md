# Longboat Key Dumpster Permit and Placement Rules

Renting a dumpster in Longboat Key, FL means knowing who governs placement, where a box can sit, and where your debris goes. This guide covers the permit question, placement, and the local Sarasota and Manatee County facilities.

## Who Governs Placement in Longboat Key

Longboat Key is an incorporated island town that straddles the Sarasota, Manatee county line, so the Town of Longboat Key governs placement and the county that handles facilities depends on where your property sits. A box on a town street or right-of-way follows town rules; a box on your own driveway generally needs none.

## Placement and Local Factors

Longboat Key is an affluent barrier island with luxury homes, condo towers and bridge access. The two-county split means you must confirm which county your property is in, and condo-building management controls placement for a unit renovation.

**On your own driveway:** a box entirely on your private driveway usually needs no permit, which is the simplest option. Protect a paver or finished driveway with boards under the roll-off's contact points.

**On the street or right-of-way:** a box there follows the rules of whoever owns the road, so confirm the current requirement before placing one.

## The Leaf It Out Yard-Waste Rule

Sarasota County's Leaf It Out rule bans plastic bags from yard-waste collection: yard waste must be in reusable containers or paper bags, within size limits, and bags brought to the landfill must be emptied. For a large landscaping tear-out beyond the curbside limits, a rented dumpster is the practical route.

## Where Your Debris Goes

Longboat Key uses the facilities of whichever county your property is in, Sarasota or Manatee. For the Sarasota side, household hazardous waste and electronics go to a Chemical Collection Center (North at 8750 Bee Ridge Road, Sarasota). Keep hazardous waste and appliances out of the dumpster, and confirm which county applies.
