# Landscaping Dumpsters: Pavers, Sod and Plants

A landscaping project mixes light plant debris with heavy sod, soil and pavers, so it balances volume and weight. This pillar post covers landscaping dumpsters.

## Landscaping Debris: Light and Heavy

A landscaping overhaul produces a mix:

- **Light, bulky:** plants, branches, brush, mulch, palm fronds, fills volume.
- **Heavy, dense:** sod, soil, pavers, rock, decorative stone, hits the weight limit fast.

So a big landscaping job often needs a general box for the plant material and a separate small box filled partway for the heavy sod, soil or pavers, to avoid an overweight mixed load. Note Sarasota County's Leaf It Out rule (no plastic bags for yard waste) affects curbside collection, but a dumpster handles a large tear-out. Describe the project, and the split of plant vs heavy material, to the hauler. See the [yard waste dumpster page](/yard-waste-dumpster-rental/).
