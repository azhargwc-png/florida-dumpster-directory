# Dumpster for Sod and Dirt Removal

Sod and dirt are among the heaviest debris, so their removal needs a small box filled partway. This post covers handling it.

## Sod and Dirt Are Heavy

Removing sod and dirt, for a regrade, a new install, or clearing an area, produces dense, heavy debris, especially when the sandy Southwest Florida soil is wet. Like concrete, it is weight-governed:

- **Use a small box filled partway.** Sod and dirt reach the weight limit fast, so a 10-yard box filled to the weight allowance, not the rim, is the tool.
- **Keep it a clean load** (just soil and sod) where possible, so it can potentially be reused as clean fill.
- **Watch wet weight.** Saturated soil after rain is much heavier.
- **Separate from light debris.** Do not mix sod and dirt into a plant-debris or general load, which it would push overweight.

Describe the volume of sod and dirt to the hauler, who sizes the small box and sets the weight allowance. See [can you put dirt in a dumpster](/blog/can-you-put-dirt-in-a-dumpster/).
