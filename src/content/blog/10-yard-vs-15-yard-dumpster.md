# 10-Yard vs 15-Yard Dumpster

The choice between a 10-yard and a 15-yard dumpster comes down to volume and debris type. The 10-yard is the heavy-debris box (concrete, tile, dirt, filled partway), while the 15-yard holds more light-to-moderate debris for a contained renovation or cleanout.

## The 10-Yard

Best for heavy debris and small light jobs. Typical jobs: concrete, tile, dirt, a single-room cleanout, a small bathroom with heavy fixtures.

## The 15-Yard

Best for a contained single-room renovation or cleanout. Typical jobs: a bathroom or small kitchen remodel, a garage or attic cleanout, a one-room flooring tear-out.

## Which to Choose

Choose the 10-yard when weight is your limiting factor, heavy material you fill partway, or when the job is small and light. Choose the 15-yard when you have more general debris than a 10-yard holds but it is a contained, single-area job. For heavy debris, the 10-yard wins regardless of volume, because weight governs.

Describe your project to the hauler, the type of job, the rooms or square footage, and the kind of debris, and they will confirm the right size. The [size calculator](/tools/dumpster-size-calculator/) is a good starting point.
