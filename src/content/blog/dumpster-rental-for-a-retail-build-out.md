# Dumpster Rental for a Retail Build-Out

A retail build-out produces demolition debris from the old space plus construction debris from the new fit-out. This post covers it.

## Retail Build-Out Debris

A retail build-out, fitting out a space for a new tenant or concept, produces:

- **Demolition debris:** old fixtures, flooring, partitions, ceiling, from stripping the space.
- **Construction debris:** drywall, flooring, fixture packaging, offcuts, from the new fit-out.

A 20- or 30-yard box with swaps handles most build-outs, with heavy material (any concrete, tile) in a separate small box. Confirm placement at the commercial site and any permit. In Lee County, C and D recycling applies. Describe the build-out scope to the hauler, who sizes the box and plans swaps across the demolition and construction phases.
