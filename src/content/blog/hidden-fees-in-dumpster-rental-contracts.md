# Hidden Fees in Dumpster Rental Contracts

Most dumpster rentals are straightforward, but a few fees can surprise people who did not ask about them. None are truly hidden; they are just easy to overlook. Knowing them lets you ask the right questions and avoid an unexpected bill.

## The Fees to Ask About

- **Weight overage.** The most common, billed per ton over the allowance.
- **Extra-day fee.** A daily rate for keeping the box past the included period.
- **Trip or dry-run fee.** Charged if the truck arrives to deliver or collect but cannot (blocked access, box not ready).
- **Permit cost.** Separate from the rental, for a street or right-of-way placement.
- **Special-item surcharge.** Some haulers add a fee for mattresses, appliances or tires.
- **Prohibited-item or contamination fee.** If a banned item is found in the load.

The fix for all of them is to ask up front what the rate is and under what conditions it applies, and to avoid the triggers (stay under weight, keep the box accessible, sort out banned items).

## Get a Real Number for Your Job

Because the total depends on your specific debris, size, days and location, the only accurate figure is a quote from a local company for your job. Describe the project, the debris, scope and location, and they account for every driver and fee so the number reflects what you will actually pay.
