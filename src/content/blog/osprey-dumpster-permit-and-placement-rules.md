# Osprey Dumpster Permit and Placement Rules

Renting a dumpster in Osprey, FL means knowing who governs placement, where a box can sit, and where your debris goes. This guide covers the permit question, placement, and the local Sarasota County facilities.

## Who Governs Placement in Osprey

Osprey is an unincorporated community in Sarasota County, so Sarasota County governs placement. A box on a public right-of-way follows county rules; a box on your own driveway generally needs no permit.

## Placement and Local Factors

Osprey is a smaller coastal community between Sarasota and Venice, with many single-family and waterfront lots that have driveway room for private placement. Confirm truck access on a waterfront or tree-covered lot.

**On your own driveway:** a box entirely on your private driveway usually needs no permit, which is the simplest option. Protect a paver or finished driveway with boards under the roll-off's contact points.

**On the street or right-of-way:** a box there follows the rules of whoever owns the road, so confirm the current requirement before placing one.

## The Leaf It Out Yard-Waste Rule

Sarasota County's Leaf It Out rule bans plastic bags from yard-waste collection: yard waste must be in reusable containers or paper bags, within size limits, and bags brought to the landfill must be emptied. For a large landscaping tear-out beyond the curbside limits, a rented dumpster is the practical route.

## Where Your Debris Goes

Osprey uses the Sarasota County facilities: household hazardous waste and electronics go free year-round to a Chemical Collection Center (North at 8750 Bee Ridge Road, Sarasota, or South at 250 S. Jackson Road, Venice), and larger debris to the county landfill at 4000 Knights Trail Road, Nokomis, or via a dumpster hauler. Keep hazardous items out of the dumpster.
