# Nokomis Dumpster Permit and Placement Rules

Renting a dumpster in Nokomis, FL means knowing who governs placement, where a box can sit, and where your debris goes. This guide covers the permit question, placement, and the local Sarasota County facilities.

## Who Governs Placement in Nokomis

Nokomis is an unincorporated community in Sarasota County, so Sarasota County governs placement. A box on a public right-of-way follows county rules; a box on your own driveway generally needs no permit.

## Placement and Local Factors

Nokomis is home to the Sarasota County landfill, the Central County Solid Waste Disposal Complex at 4000 Knights Trail Road, which is convenient for self-haul. Many Nokomis lots have driveway room for private placement.

**On your own driveway:** a box entirely on your private driveway usually needs no permit, which is the simplest option. Protect a paver or finished driveway with boards under the roll-off's contact points.

**On the street or right-of-way:** a box there follows the rules of whoever owns the road, so confirm the current requirement before placing one.

## The Leaf It Out Yard-Waste Rule

Sarasota County's Leaf It Out rule bans plastic bags from yard-waste collection: yard waste must be in reusable containers or paper bags, within size limits, and bags brought to the landfill must be emptied. For a large landscaping tear-out beyond the curbside limits, a rented dumpster is the practical route.

## Where Your Debris Goes

Nokomis is home to the Sarasota County landfill at 4000 Knights Trail Road, which has a Citizens' Convenience Center for resident disposal. Household hazardous waste and electronics go to a Chemical Collection Center (the North center at 8750 Bee Ridge Road, Sarasota, or South at 250 S. Jackson Road, Venice). Keep hazardous items out of the dumpster.

**Is the Sarasota County landfill in Nokomis?**
Yes. The Central County Solid Waste Disposal Complex, the Sarasota County landfill, is at 4000 Knights Trail Road, Nokomis, and includes a Citizens' Convenience Center for resident disposal. This makes self-haul convenient for Nokomis residents, though for most renovations and cleanouts a dumpster hauler routes the debris to the facility for you. Hazardous waste and electronics still go to a Chemical Collection Center, not the landfill, and follow the Leaf It Out rule for yard waste.

Heavy debris like concrete or tile goes in a smaller 10-yard box filled partway, since weight hits the limit before the box looks full.
