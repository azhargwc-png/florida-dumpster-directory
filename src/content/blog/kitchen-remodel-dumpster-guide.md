# Kitchen Remodel Dumpster Guide

A kitchen remodel produces bulky cabinetry and, often, heavy stone countertops, so it balances volume and weight. This post covers sizing and handling.

## Kitchen Remodel Debris

A kitchen gut produces cabinets, countertops, appliances, flooring, backsplash tile, drywall and fixtures. The balance:

- **Cabinets drive the volume.** Bulky but light; break them down to pack efficiently.
- **Stone countertops drive the weight.** Granite and quartz are heavy, so flag them to the hauler.
- **Size:** a 15- or 20-yard box fits most kitchen remodels; a full gut with flooring and tile leans to the 20-yard.
- **Appliances:** route refrigerant appliances for recovery; others may be white goods kept separate.
- **Backsplash and floor tile** add weight.

See [what size dumpster for a kitchen remodel](/blog/what-size-dumpster-for-a-kitchen-remodel/). Describe the kitchen, and flag stone countertops and appliances, to the hauler.
