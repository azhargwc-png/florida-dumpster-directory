# Sanibel Dumpster Permit and Placement Rules

Renting a dumpster in Sanibel, FL means knowing who governs placement, where a box can sit, and where your debris goes. This guide covers the permit question, placement, and the local Lee County facilities.

## Who Governs Placement in Sanibel

Sanibel is an incorporated island city in Lee County with its own strict environmental and placement standards, so the City of Sanibel governs placement. A box on a city street or right-of-way needs a city permit; a box on your own driveway generally needs none.

## Placement and Local Factors

Sanibel is a barrier island reached over a causeway, with strict environmental rules and tight lots, and it is rebuilding from recent storms. Plan island access and placement carefully, and confirm any island-specific requirements with the city.

**On your own driveway:** a box entirely on your private driveway usually needs no permit, which is the simplest option. Protect a paver or finished driveway with boards under the roll-off's contact points.

**On the street or right-of-way:** a box there follows the rules of whoever owns the road, so confirm the current requirement before placing one.

## Where Your Debris Goes

Sanibel uses the Lee County facilities on the mainland: household hazardous waste and electronics go free to the Topaz Court facility at 6441 Topaz Court, Fort Myers. The county's mandatory C and D recycling applies. Keep hazardous waste, electronics and appliances out of the dumpster, and plan the causeway trip.
