# Before, During and After Delivery Checklist

A dumpster rental goes smoothly with a simple checklist for each stage. This pillar post covers before, during and after delivery.

## The Three-Stage Checklist

**Before delivery:** size the box to your debris, choose a firm driveway spot, confirm any permit, clear the spot and approach, check overhead, lay boards on a paver or finished driveway, and mark the placement.

**During the rental:** load big flat items first, fill gaps, break down bulky items, keep heavy debris within the weight allowance, keep banned items out, and keep the load level with the fill line.

**After (pickup):** make sure the box is level with the fill line and accessible, confirm banned items are out, and call for pickup when done or arrange an extension before the period ends.

Working through each stage prevents the common problems, wrong size, overfilling, overage, trip fees, and keeps the rental to its quote. Describe your job to the hauler, who helps with the sizing.
