# Why Heavy Debris Fills a Small Dumpster

A box of concrete can be too heavy to haul while looking nearly empty. This counterintuitive fact drives how heavy debris is sized. This post explains it.

## Heavy Means Full by Weight, Not Volume

Dense materials, concrete, brick, tile, dirt, sand, stone, weigh so much per cubic foot that a small amount uses up the weight allowance while taking little space. So a 10-yard box of concrete can reach its weight limit when it is only a quarter or a third full by volume. The box 'fills' by weight long before it fills by volume.

This is why heavy debris goes in a small box filled only partway: a larger box would just let you add more weight than the truck can safely lift. The small box, filled to the weight allowance, is the right tool. It also explains why a heavy load that looks far from full is still at its limit, and why you should never judge a heavy load by how full it looks. Describe heavy debris to the hauler, who sets the box and allowance by weight.
