# Dumpster for Flood Damage Cleanup

Flood damage cleanup generates heavy, wet debris, and a dumpster handles the ruined materials you tear out. The main factors are weight and keeping hazardous and restricted items out. This post covers it.

## Handling Wet Flood Debris

Flooding ruins drywall, flooring, carpet, insulation, cabinetry and contents, and all of it comes out wet and heavy. For a flood cleanup:

- **Expect weight.** Wet drywall, carpet and soaked materials weigh far more than dry, so the load reaches the weight allowance faster. Tell the hauler it is flood debris so the box and allowance fit.
- **Tear out to the flood line,** typically removing wet drywall and insulation above the water line to prevent mold.
- **Keep hazardous items out.** Flood-contaminated chemicals, paint and fuel go to the county facility; appliances with refrigerant need recovery.
- **Separate contents from construction debris** if your county's storm program is collecting eligible debris.

A private dumpster handles the private-property flood debris you are tearing out, distinct from any county curbside storm collection.
