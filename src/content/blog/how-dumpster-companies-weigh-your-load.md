# How Dumpster Companies Weigh Your Load

Your dumpster load is weighed at the disposal facility, and that weight determines part of your cost through the weight allowance and any overage. This post explains how the weighing works.

## How Weighing Works

When the hauler takes your box to the landfill or transfer station, the loaded truck is weighed, and after dumping, the empty truck is weighed again; the difference is your load's weight. The facility charges the hauler by that weight (a tipping fee), and that flows into your rental:

- **Your rental includes a weight allowance** (in tons) within the base price.
- **If the load exceeds it,** the excess is billed per ton as an overage.

This is why dense debris (concrete, tile, dirt) costs more: it reaches the allowance fast. The weighing is done at the scale at the facility, not at your site, so you see the result as the final weight-based cost. To stay within the allowance, keep heavy material in a small box filled partway and describe heavy debris when you book.
