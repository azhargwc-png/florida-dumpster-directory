# Clean Concrete vs Mixed Debris Loads

A clean concrete load recycles and manages weight; a mixed load does neither well. This post explains the difference and why separating concrete pays off.

## Clean vs Mixed

**A clean concrete load** is concrete and masonry only, no wood, trash or contamination. It can be crushed and recycled into aggregate, is easier for the hauler to price and handle, and keeps the dense material in a small box sized for its weight.

**A mixed load** combines concrete with general debris. The problems: the heavy concrete can push the whole load overweight (an overage charge), and the contamination means the concrete can no longer be cleanly recycled, so the load heads to disposal.

So for anything more than a trace of concrete, keep it in its own clean load. The modest cost of a second small box is usually less than an overweight overage, and it supports recycling, which matters especially in Lee County with its C and D recycling requirement. Describe both debris streams to the hauler.
