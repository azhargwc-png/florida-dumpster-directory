# Dumpster Rental for a Business Cleanout

A business cleanout, clearing an office, store or warehouse, produces furniture, fixtures, inventory and general debris. This post covers it.

## Clearing a Business Space

A business cleanout, for a move, closure, renovation or decluttering, produces office furniture, shelving and fixtures, old inventory, and general debris. To handle it:

- **Size by volume** (mostly bulky light debris): a 20-yard for a typical office or store, more for a warehouse or large space.
- **Set aside electronics** (computers, monitors) for e-waste, and any hazardous materials for the county facility.
- **Confirm placement** at a commercial site, and any permit for a street spot.
- **Consider donating** usable furniture and fixtures.

Describe the space and contents to the hauler, who sizes the box and plans swaps for a large cleanout.
