# LaBelle Dumpster Permit and Placement Rules

Renting a dumpster in LaBelle, FL means knowing who governs placement, where a box can sit, and where your debris goes. This guide covers the permit question, placement, and the local Hendry County facilities.

## Who Governs Placement in LaBelle

LaBelle is an incorporated city and the Hendry County seat, so the City of LaBelle governs inside the city and Hendry County governs the unincorporated areas, including Port LaBelle and rural lots. A box on your own property generally needs no permit; a street box follows the applicable rules.

## Placement and Local Factors

LaBelle is a rural agricultural city on the Caloosahatchee River, so dumpster jobs often involve modest-home renovation, mobile-home work, and agricultural and land cleanups. Hendry County's small curbside construction-debris limit means most real projects need a dumpster from the start.

**On your own driveway:** a box entirely on your private driveway usually needs no permit, which is the simplest option. Protect a paver or finished driveway with boards under the roll-off's contact points.

**On the street or right-of-way:** a box there follows the rules of whoever owns the road, so confirm the current requirement before placing one.

## Where Your Debris Goes

LaBelle uses the Hendry County arrangements. The county's curbside construction-debris limit is small (about 2 cubic yards per month), so larger amounts go to a transfer station or a rented dumpster; the regional Lee/Hendry landfill serves the area. Hazardous waste goes to Hendry County's household hazardous waste collection. Keep hazardous waste and appliances out of the dumpster, and describe agricultural debris to the hauler.

It depends on the job and the debris weight. A cleanout or shingle re-roof usually fits a 20-yard box, a bathroom remodel a 15-yard, and a whole-home renovation a 20- or 30-yard. Heavy debris like concrete or tile goes in a smaller 10-yard box filled partway, since weight hits the limit before the box looks full. Confirm the size by weight with a dumpster company serving LaBelle, and keep hazardous waste, electronics and appliances out of the box.
