# Dumpster for a Downsizing Move

Downsizing means parting with a lot, and a dumpster handles what is not worth keeping, selling or donating. This post covers a downsizing cleanout, common among Southwest Florida's retirees.

## Clearing for a Smaller Home

Downsizing, common in Southwest Florida's large retiree population, means moving to a smaller home and parting with belongings that will not fit. A dumpster handles the discards:

- **Sort first.** Keep what fits the new home, give to family, sell valuable items, and donate usable furniture and goods.
- **Dumpster the rest.** Worn furniture, broken items, accumulated goods and general junk go in the box.
- **Size by volume.** A 20-yard box fits most downsizing cleanouts, since the debris is bulky household goods; a large long-held home may need a 30-yard or a swap.
- **Set aside hazardous items and electronics** for the county facility.

Downsizing is often emotional and done on a timeline (a home sale, a move-in date), so time the box to the sorting. Describe the home to the hauler for a size.
