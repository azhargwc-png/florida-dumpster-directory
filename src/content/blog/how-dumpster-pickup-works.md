# How Dumpster Pickup Works

Pickup is the reverse of delivery: the truck returns, loads the box and hauls it to the disposal facility. Having the box ready makes it quick and avoids a wasted trip.

## How Pickup Works

When you are done loading, or the rental period ends, you call the company to schedule pickup. The truck returns, the driver tilts the bed and winches the box back on, tarps the load, and hauls it to the landfill or transfer station, where it is weighed and emptied. To have it ready:

- **Keep the load level with the fill line,** not heaped above it, so it can be legally hauled and tarped.
- **Keep the box accessible,** with a clear approach, so the truck can reach it.
- **Have banned items out,** so there is no contamination issue.

If the box cannot be collected (overfilled, blocked), the trip can fail and incur a fee, so get it ready before you schedule. If you need more time, arrange an extension before the period ends.
