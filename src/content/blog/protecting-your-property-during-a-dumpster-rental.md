# Protecting Your Property During a Dumpster Rental

Beyond the driveway, a dumpster rental can affect landscaping, utilities and the house if not placed carefully. This post is the whole-property protection checklist.

## Whole-Property Protection

Protecting your property during a rental goes beyond the driveway:

- **Driveway:** boards under the box's rails and the truck path (see the paver and concrete posts).
- **Lawn and landscaping:** keep the box and truck off the lawn, beds and plantings; the truck's approach should avoid them.
- **Overhead:** clear low branches so the truck's tilting bed does not tear them, and keep clear of wires.
- **Underground:** keep the box off septic components, irrigation lines and shallow utilities (see the utilities post).
- **The house and structures:** leave room so the box and truck do not scrape the house, garage, or a gate.
- **Edges and culverts:** protect the driveway apron and swale culvert from the truck.

The common thread is placement: a well-chosen firm driveway spot, cleared and boarded, protects most of the property at once. Describe your property and placement to the hauler, who can flag risks.
