# Mistakes That Lead to Overage Fees

Overage fees almost always come from a few specific weight mistakes. This post lists them and the fix for each.

## The Overage Mistakes

- **Heavy debris in a big box.** Concrete, tile or dirt in a 20- or 30-yard reaches the weight limit while the box looks empty. Fix: use a small box filled partway for dense material.
- **Mixing heavy into a light load.** A little concrete or tile in a general load quietly pushes it overweight. Fix: separate heavy material.
- **Underestimating weight.** Not telling the hauler about heavy or wet debris means the allowance is too low. Fix: describe heavy and wet material up front.
- **Topping off a heavy box.** Filling a heavy load to the rim instead of the weight line. Fix: follow the hauler's fill guidance.
- **Wet debris.** Soaked carpet, drywall and insulation weigh far more than dry. Fix: flag wet debris, or let it dry.

Every overage traces to loading more weight than the allowance covers, so the fixes all come down to matching the box and load to the weight.
