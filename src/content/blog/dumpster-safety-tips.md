# Dumpster Safety Tips

A dumpster on site is safe with a few sensible practices. This pillar post covers dumpster safety.

## Staying Safe Around a Dumpster

Key safety practices:

- **Lift safely.** Bend your knees, get help for heavy items, and do not overexert.
- **Distribute weight evenly** across the box floor, not piled at one end.
- **Do not climb in** to compact debris, especially on construction debris with nails and sharp edges.
- **Keep the load level** with the fill line, not heaped above, so nothing falls out.
- **Watch for nails and sharp edges;** wear gloves and sturdy footwear.
- **Keep hazardous items out** (no fire risk, no chemicals).
- **Keep kids and pets away** from the box and the work area.
- **Clear the area for the truck** at delivery and pickup.

Most dumpster injuries come from unsafe lifting, climbing in, or sharp debris, all avoidable with care. Describe your debris to the hauler for the right box, which also helps you load safely.
