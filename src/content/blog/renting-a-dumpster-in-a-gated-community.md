# Renting a Dumpster in a Gated Community

A gated community adds gate access and often stricter rules to a dumpster rental. This post covers it.

## Dumpsters in a Gated Community

In a gated community, common across affluent Southwest Florida, a dumpster rental needs extra coordination:

- **Gate access:** the delivery truck needs to get through the gate, so arrange a gate code, a call-ahead, or a guard notification.
- **Placement rules:** often driveway only, with the association's limits on duration and screening.
- **Delivery hours:** gated communities may restrict when trucks can come.
- **Notice:** some require notifying the association or gate in advance.

So confirm the gate access method and the community's rules before booking, and give the hauler the gate details so delivery and pickup are not turned away. A box on your driveway still generally avoids a government permit, but the community rules apply. Describe the community to the hauler.
