# Using Boards Under a Dumpster

Boards under a dumpster are the single most effective way to protect a driveway. This post covers what to use and where to put them.

## What Boards and Where

Boards spread a dumpster's concentrated weight across more surface, so no single point (a paver, a slab section) takes the full load, and they keep the box's metal off a finished surface. What to use and where:

- **What:** sturdy two-by-ten planks or sheets of 3/4-inch plywood. They must be strong enough not to crack under the load.
- **Under the box:** place boards where the box's rails will rest, running the length of the contact points.
- **Along the truck path:** on a delicate surface, lay boards where the truck's wheels will roll, since the truck is heaviest.
- **Have them down first:** lay the boards before the truck arrives, and ask the hauler to set the box on them.

Boards are cheap and reusable, and they prevent the paver, concrete and asphalt damage a loaded box can otherwise cause. They are the core of driveway protection.
