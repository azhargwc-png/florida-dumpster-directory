# Dumpster on the Street vs Driveway

You can place a dumpster on your driveway or, with a permit, on the street. Each has trade-offs around permits, access and your property. This post weighs them.

## Driveway vs Street

**Driveway placement** is usually simplest: generally no permit, the box is on your property, and it is convenient for loading. The trade-off is that it ties up the driveway and, on a paver or finished surface, needs board protection.

**Street or right-of-way placement** frees the driveway, but generally needs a permit from the city or county, may have a time limit set by that permit, and must not obstruct traffic or drainage. In most Southwest Florida communities the swale is public right-of-way, so a box there follows the street rule.

For most homeowners, the driveway is the easier choice. Use the street when the driveway cannot fit the box or must stay clear, and pull the permit first. A local hauler can advise which placement suits your lot and whether a permit is needed.
