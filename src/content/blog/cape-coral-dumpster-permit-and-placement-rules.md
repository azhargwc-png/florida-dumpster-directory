# Cape Coral Dumpster Permit and Placement Rules

Renting a dumpster in Cape Coral, FL means knowing who governs placement, where a box can sit, and where your debris goes. This guide covers the permit question, placement, and the local Lee County facilities.

## Who Governs Placement in Cape Coral

Cape Coral is an incorporated city in Lee County, so the City of Cape Coral governs its streets and rights-of-way. A box on a city street or right-of-way typically needs a city permit; a box on your own driveway generally needs none.

## Placement and Local Factors

Cape Coral is heavily platted with canal lots, so confirm truck access and placement on a tight waterfront or canal lot. It is one of the fastest-growing cities in the region, with steady renovation and new-build demand.

**On your own driveway:** a box entirely on your private driveway usually needs no permit, which is the simplest option. Protect a paver or finished driveway with boards under the roll-off's contact points.

**On the street or right-of-way:** a box there follows the rules of whoever owns the road, so confirm the current requirement before placing one.

## Where Your Debris Goes

Cape Coral residents use the Lee County Topaz Court facility at 6441 Topaz Court, Fort Myers for household hazardous waste and electronics, free for residents. The county's mandatory C and D recycling applies. Keep hazardous waste, electronics and appliances out of the dumpster.

**Where does hazardous waste go in Cape Coral?**
Cape Coral residents take household hazardous waste to Lee County's permanent facility at 6441 Topaz Court, Fort Myers, which is free for residents and also takes electronics. It accepts paint, solvents, pesticides, pool chemicals, oil, batteries and more. None of these go in a dumpster, so set them aside before loading a roll-off for a Cape Coral renovation or cleanout. For a canal or waterfront lot, also confirm truck access and placement with the hauler.

**What size dumpster do I need in Cape Coral?**
It depends on the job and the debris weight. A cleanout or shingle re-roof usually fits a 20-yard box, a bathroom remodel a 15-yard, and a whole-home renovation a 20- or 30-yard. Heavy debris like concrete or tile goes in a smaller 10-yard box filled partway, since weight hits the limit before the box looks full. Confirm the size by weight with a dumpster company serving Cape Coral, and keep hazardous waste, electronics and appliances out of the box.
