# Dumpster Rules in Deed-Restricted Communities

Deed-restricted communities carry rules recorded on the property that can govern dumpster rental. This post explains them.

## What Deed Restrictions Add

A deed-restricted community has rules recorded on the properties (enforced by an association) that owners agree to, and these can include dumpster-related restrictions:

- **Placement and duration** limits.
- **Screening** requirements.
- **Approval or notice** for temporary containers.
- **Delivery timing.**

These apply in addition to, and sometimes more strictly than, the city or county rules. Rotonda West is a Southwest Florida example that layers deed restrictions over the county swale rule. So in any deed-restricted community, check the recorded restrictions or the association's rules before booking, and plan placement and timing to comply. A driveway box generally avoids a government permit, but the deed restrictions apply regardless. Describe the community to the hauler.
