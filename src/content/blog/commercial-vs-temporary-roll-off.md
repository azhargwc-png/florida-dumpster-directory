# Commercial vs Temporary Roll-Off

Businesses use two kinds of dumpster service: permanent commercial containers and temporary roll-offs. They serve different needs. This pillar post explains the difference.

## Permanent vs Temporary

**A commercial (permanent) container** is an ongoing bin, often a front-load dumpster, emptied on a regular route for a business's day-to-day waste. It stays on site indefinitely.

**A temporary roll-off** is rented for a specific project, a renovation, a cleanout, a build-out, then hauled away when done.

So a business uses a permanent container for routine operations and a temporary roll-off for a one-time project that produces debris the routine service cannot handle. This directory connects you with companies for temporary roll-off rentals. Describe the project to the hauler for a size.
