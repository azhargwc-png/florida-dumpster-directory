# Interior Demolition Dumpster Guide

Interior demolition, taking a space down to the studs, produces a burst of mixed debris. This post covers sizing and handling an interior demo.

## Interior Demolition Debris

An interior demolition, a gut of a room, floor or whole interior, produces drywall, flooring, cabinetry, fixtures, trim, and sometimes tile and masonry, all at once:

- **Size for the burst.** A single room fits a 15- or 20-yard; a whole-interior gut a 20- or 30-yard, or swaps.
- **Separate heavy material.** Tile, concrete and masonry go in a small box filled partway.
- **Mind drywall weight.** A full gut generates a lot of drywall, which is denser than it looks.
- **Check pre-1980s homes for asbestos** before demo (popcorn ceilings, old tile).
- **Route the exceptions** to the county facility.

See the [demolition dumpster page](/demolition-dumpster-rental/). Describe the demo scope to the hauler, who sizes the box and plans swaps.
