# Delivery Without a Driveway

If you have no driveway, or it cannot take a box, a dumpster can still be delivered, usually to the street or swale with a permit. This pillar post covers the options.

## Options Without a Driveway

No driveway, or one that cannot fit a box, does not rule out a rental:

- **Street or right-of-way placement.** The usual alternative, with a permit from the city or county (in most Southwest Florida communities the swale is right-of-way too). Follow the permit's conditions.
- **A smaller box** that fits a tight spot or a short drive.
- **Shared or alternative placement** in a community (with approval).
- **Junk removal** as an alternative for some jobs, a crew hauls without placing a box.

So confirm the street/swale permit requirement with the city or county, or ask the hauler, and plan placement that does not obstruct traffic, drainage or access. Describe your situation to the hauler, who can advise on placement and the permit.
