# Can You Put a Mattress in a Dumpster?

Mattresses are one of the most common things people want to throw in a rental dumpster, and the answer is usually yes, with a couple of caveats. Mattresses are bulky, hard to compact, and some haulers charge a small handling fee because they are difficult to process at the landfill. This post covers whether a mattress can go in your dumpster, how it affects your space, and the alternatives for just one or two.

## Yes, Usually, But Ask First

Most roll-off dumpster rentals accept mattresses and box springs as part of a general load. They are not hazardous and not banned, so a mattress from a cleanout or bedroom remodel can normally go in the box. The caveat is that many haulers and landfills treat mattresses as a special item, because the springs and foam are hard to compact and process. Some charge a small per-mattress handling fee, and a few ask that mattresses be kept separate. Always ask the hauler about their mattress policy when you book, so there are no surprises.

## They Eat Space

A mattress does not compact, so it takes up its full volume in the box and resists being packed around. A queen or king mattress plus a box spring can swallow a surprising share of a small dumpster. If your project is mostly mattresses (a rental turnover or a dorm cleanout, say), factor that bulk into the size you choose, since you cannot crush them down to make room.

## The Alternatives for One or Two

If you only have a mattress or two and no other debris, a full roll-off may be more than you need. Options include a bulk pickup through your regular hauler where offered, a mattress-recycling drop-off, or a retailer haul-away when a new mattress is delivered (many will take the old one). A dumpster makes sense when the mattress is part of a larger cleanout or renovation, not as a standalone for a single item.

**Is there a fee for mattresses in a dumpster?**
There can be. Not every hauler charges it, and policies vary, so the only way to know is to ask when you book. If you have several mattresses, mention the count up front so the quote reflects them and you choose a box with room for their bulk.
