# How to Prepare for a Dumpster Delivery

A few minutes of preparation makes dumpster delivery quick, protects your property, and avoids a wasted-trip fee. This post is the preparation checklist.

## The Preparation Checklist

- **Clear the spot.** Move vehicles and obstacles from where the box will sit and the truck's approach.
- **Check overhead.** Make sure there are no low branches, wires or a low roofline over the spot or approach, since the truck's bed tilts high.
- **Protect the surface.** Lay boards (plywood or two-by-tens) under where the box will rest on a paver, stamped or finished driveway.
- **Mark the placement.** Use cones, tape or a note to show the exact spot and orientation you want (door-end toward your work).
- **Confirm access.** Open any gate, and make sure the approach is firm, especially on an unpaved drive.
- **Know the rules.** Confirm whether a street or swale placement needs a permit; keep it on the driveway if you want to avoid one.

With the spot ready, delivery takes minutes and the box lands exactly where you need it.
