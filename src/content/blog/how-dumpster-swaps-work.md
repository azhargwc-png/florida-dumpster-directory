# How Dumpster Swaps Work

A swap is when the hauler collects your full box and drops an empty one in its place. It keeps a big or phased job moving without a long gap. This post explains how swaps work.

## What a Swap Is

On a swap, the truck hauls away your full box and leaves an empty one on the same spot, so you can keep loading. It is an alternative to one very large box, and it is useful when:

- **The job produces more debris than one box holds,** such as a whole-home gut or a large cleanout.
- **Access limits the box size,** so you use a smaller box but need more than one load.
- **You want to separate loads,** for example a heavy clean-concrete load then a light general load.
- **The job runs in phases,** so you fill, swap, and keep going.

Each swapped box is hauled, weighed and emptied like a normal pickup, so each has its own weight allowance. Ask the hauler how swaps are scheduled and priced for your project.
