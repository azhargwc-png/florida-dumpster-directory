# What Drives Dumpster Rental Cost

Dumpster rental is priced per job from a handful of cost drivers. Knowing them tells you why quotes differ and how to describe your job for an accurate one. No two jobs price the same, so the drivers, not a fixed price, are what to understand.

## The Six Cost Drivers

- **Size.** A larger box costs more, though heavy debris still goes in a small box for weight.
- **Weight.** Most rentals include a weight allowance; exceeding it adds a per-ton overage.
- **Rental period.** The base covers set days; extra days add a daily rate.
- **Debris type.** Clean single-material loads can be handled differently from mixed debris.
- **Distance.** Delivery and haul distance from the yard affects cost, so remote or island locations may cost more.
- **Disposal and permits.** Landfill tipping fees (charged by weight) and any right-of-way permit add to the total.

## Get a Real Number for Your Job

Because the total depends on your specific debris, size, days and location, the only accurate figure is a quote from a local company for your job. Describe the project, the debris, scope and location, and they account for every driver and fee so the number reflects what you will actually pay.
