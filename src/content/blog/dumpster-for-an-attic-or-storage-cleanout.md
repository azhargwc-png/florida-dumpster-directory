# Dumpster for an Attic or Storage Cleanout

Attics and storage units accumulate boxes, old furniture and forgotten items. A dumpster clears them efficiently. This post covers an attic or storage cleanout.

## Clearing Stored Clutter

Attics, storage units and storage rooms hold boxes, old furniture, holiday decorations, documents and forgotten items, mostly light and bulky. A dumpster clears the volume:

- **Size by volume.** A 10- or 15-yard box fits most attic or single storage-unit cleanouts; a large or packed space may need a 20-yard.
- **Watch for a few heavy surprises:** old electronics (to e-waste), paint or chemicals stored away (to the county facility), and the occasional heavy item.
- **Check for old insulation** in an attic; if the home is pre-1980s and you are disturbing it, consider whether asbestos testing is warranted.
- **Sort for documents and valuables** before dumping, since stored boxes can hold both.

Donate usable items. Describe the space to the hauler for a size.
