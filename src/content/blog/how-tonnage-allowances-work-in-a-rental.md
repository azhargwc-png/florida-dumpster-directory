# How Tonnage Allowances Work in a Rental

The tonnage allowance is the amount of debris weight included in your rental's base price. Understanding it is the key to weight-based cost. This post explains it.

## What the Tonnage Allowance Covers

Your rental's base price includes a tonnage allowance, a number of tons of debris weight. It works like this:

- **Within the allowance,** the disposal is covered by the base price.
- **Above the allowance,** the excess is billed per ton, the overage rate.

The allowance is set by the box size and the company, and reflects the tipping fee the hauler expects to pay at the disposal facility. It is separate from the volume: you can hit the tonnage allowance while the box looks only partly full, especially with dense material. Because the allowance varies by size and company, confirm it when you book, and describe heavy debris so the hauler sets an allowance that fits the job. Staying within it, by keeping heavy material in a small box filled partway, keeps the final bill matching the quote.
