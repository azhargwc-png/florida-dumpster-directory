# How Much Does a Roof Replacement Dumpster Hold?

How much a roofing dumpster holds depends on the roof type, because weight, not volume, usually governs a roofing load. This post explains the capacity.

## Capacity by Roof Type

A roofing dumpster's capacity is set by weight as much as volume:

- **Shingle:** a 20-yard box holds a typical single-family shingle roof, since asphalt is moderately heavy and you can use much of the volume.
- **Tile:** a 10-yard box filled partway holds far fewer squares, because concrete or clay tile reaches the weight limit quickly. The box looks only partly full at its weight ceiling.

So a roofing dumpster 'holds' as many squares as the weight allowance permits, which is many more for shingle than for tile. Multiple old layers reduce the capacity further. Give the hauler the roof type, square footage and layer count, and they will confirm whether one box holds your tear-off or you need a swap.
