# Sarasota Dumpster Permit and Placement Rules

Renting a dumpster in Sarasota, FL means confirming whether the City of Sarasota or Sarasota County governs your address, and knowing the county's Leaf It Out yard-waste rule and Chemical Collection Center. This guide covers permits, placement, and where your debris goes.

## City of Sarasota vs County

The Sarasota area splits between the incorporated City of Sarasota and the surrounding unincorporated Sarasota County. A box on a city street or right-of-way follows City of Sarasota rules; a box in an unincorporated area follows county rules; and a box on your own driveway generally needs no permit either way. Confirm which governs your address before placing a box on the street.

## Where Your Debris Goes

Sarasota uses the Sarasota County facilities. Household hazardous waste and electronics go free year-round to the Chemical Collection Center (North) at 8750 Bee Ridge Road, Sarasota, the closest center. Larger debris goes to the county landfill at 4000 Knights Trail Road, Nokomis, or via a dumpster hauler. The City of Sarasota also partners with the county on periodic hazardous-waste collection events. Keep hazardous waste, electronics and appliances out of the dumpster.

## The Leaf It Out Yard-Waste Rule

Sarasota County's Leaf It Out rule bans plastic bags from yard-waste collection: yard waste must be in reusable containers or paper bags, within size limits, and bags brought to the landfill must be emptied. For a large landscaping tear-out beyond the curbside limits, a rented dumpster is the practical route. This matters for a Sarasota property with mature landscaping being overhauled.

## Driveway and Older Neighborhoods

Sarasota's older neighborhoods can have narrower lots and mature tree cover, so confirm the delivery truck's access and the placement spot. Protect a paver or finished driveway with boards under the roll-off's contact points.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need a permit for a dumpster in Sarasota?**
It depends on where the box sits and who governs your address. A box on your own driveway generally needs no permit. A box on a public street or right-of-way needs a permit from the City of Sarasota if you are inside the city, or Sarasota County if you are in an unincorporated area, so confirm which applies. Older Sarasota neighborhoods can have narrow lots, so also confirm the delivery truck's access and placement before booking.

**Where does hazardous waste go in Sarasota?**
To a Sarasota County Chemical Collection Center, free for residents year-round. The closest is the North center at 8750 Bee Ridge Road, Sarasota, which takes hazardous materials and electronics, including paint, solvents, pesticides, pool chemicals and batteries. Pharmaceuticals are no longer accepted there, so use a prescription-drug drop-off instead. The City of Sarasota also partners with the county on periodic collection events. None of these items go in a dumpster, so set them aside before loading a roll-off.

**What is the Leaf It Out rule in Sarasota?**
Leaf It Out is Sarasota County's rule banning plastic bags from yard-waste disposal. Yard waste must be in reusable containers or paper bags, within size limits (under 4 feet, under 40 pounds, 16 inches or less in diameter), and bags brought to the landfill must be emptied. For a large landscaping tear-out beyond those curbside limits, a rented dumpster is the practical route rather than many small bagged loads. This applies to a Sarasota property having its mature landscaping overhauled.

**What size dumpster do I need in Sarasota?**
It depends on the job and the debris weight. A cleanout or shingle re-roof usually fits a 20-yard box, a bathroom remodel a 15-yard, and a whole-home renovation a 20- or 30-yard. Heavy debris like tile, concrete or masonry goes in a smaller 10-yard box filled partway, since weight hits the limit before the box looks full. For a large landscaping job, size for volume. Confirm the size by weight with a dumpster company serving Sarasota.
