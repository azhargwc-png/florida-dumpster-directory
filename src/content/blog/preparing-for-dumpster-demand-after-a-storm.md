# Preparing for Dumpster Demand After a Storm

Dumpster demand spikes after a hurricane, so planning ahead helps you get a box when you need one. This post covers how to prepare for the post-storm surge.

## Planning Around the Surge

After a storm, everyone needs debris handling at once, so boxes and trucks are scarce and lead times stretch. To prepare:

- **Book as early as you can** once you know you will need a box for rebuild work.
- **Use the county curbside program** for eligible storm debris set at the right-of-way, which does not depend on renting a box and frees your dumpster need for rebuild debris only.
- **Separate debris correctly** so the county takes the eligible material and your dumpster handles only what it should.
- **Have placement ready,** since access may be complicated by storm debris around your property.
- **Expect weight,** since wet and dense storm debris is heavy.

Planning this way gets the most out of both the county program and a private dumpster during the busy period.
