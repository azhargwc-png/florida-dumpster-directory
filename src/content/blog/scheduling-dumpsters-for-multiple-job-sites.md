# Scheduling Dumpsters for Multiple Job Sites

A contractor or manager running several sites can coordinate dumpster service across them with one hauler. This post covers scheduling.

## Coordinating Across Sites

Running multiple job sites, a single local hauler can coordinate the whole dumpster operation:

- **Deliveries** timed to each site's start.
- **Swaps** scheduled to each site's debris flow, so no box overfills or sits idle.
- **Pickups** as each job finishes.
- **Consistent handling** of heavy-material separation and (in Lee County) C and D recycling across sites.

Coordinating with one hauler is simpler than separate arrangements per site, and lets the hauler optimize routing. Describe the sites, their schedules and debris types to the hauler, who plans the box sizes and swap timing across the portfolio.
