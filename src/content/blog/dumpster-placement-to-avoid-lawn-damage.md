# Dumpster Placement to Avoid Lawn Damage

The surest way to avoid lawn damage is to keep the dumpster on the driveway, but if grass is unavoidable, boards help. This post covers protecting the lawn.

## Keeping the Lawn Intact

A loaded dumpster and the delivery truck are heavy enough to rut, compact and kill grass, and on soft Florida ground they can sink. The best protection is simple:

- **Use the driveway.** A firm driveway placement avoids lawn damage entirely, and most lots have driveway room.
- **If grass is unavoidable,** lay plywood under the box's rails and along the truck's path to spread the load, choose the firmest, most level grass, keep the box smaller, and avoid placement after heavy rain when the ground is saturated.
- **Expect some impact** on grass even with boards, since the weight and the truck's wheels press into the lawn.

So the driveway is strongly preferred; grass placement risks ruts, bare patches and a stuck truck, and should be a last resort with board protection. Describe the ground to the hauler if you must use grass, so they judge whether the truck can place and retrieve the box safely.
