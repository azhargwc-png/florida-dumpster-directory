# What Is a Dumpster Weight Overage Charge?

Most dumpster rentals include a weight allowance, and going over it triggers an overage charge billed per ton. It is the most common surprise on a dumpster bill, and it is avoidable once you understand it.

## How the Weight Allowance Works

A rental includes a weight allowance (a number of tons) within the base price. The disposal facility weighs the loaded box, and if it exceeds the allowance, the hauler bills the excess per ton, since the landfill charges them by weight (tipping fees). Heavy debris, concrete, tile, dirt, stone, reaches the allowance fast, which is why those materials go in a small box filled partway. Light debris rarely hits the allowance. To avoid an overage, match the box and load to the weight: describe heavy debris up front, keep heavy material in its own small box filled partway, and do not top a box off past the hauler's fill guidance.

## Get a Real Number for Your Job

Because the total depends on your specific debris, size, days and location, the only accurate figure is a quote from a local company for your job. Describe the project, the debris, scope and location, and they account for every driver and fee so the number reflects what you will actually pay.
