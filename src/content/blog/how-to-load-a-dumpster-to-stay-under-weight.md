# How to Load a Dumpster to Stay Under Weight

How you load a dumpster affects whether you stay under the weight allowance. A few habits keep a load within its limit. This post covers them.

## Loading to Stay Under Weight

To keep a load within its weight allowance:

- **Separate heavy material.** Put concrete, tile, dirt and masonry in a dedicated small box filled partway, not in the general debris box.
- **Follow the fill guidance for heavy loads,** which is often a line partway up the box, not the rim.
- **Keep wet debris out where possible,** or let it dry, since water adds weight; flag unavoidable wet debris to the hauler.
- **Distribute weight evenly** across the box floor for safe lifting, not piled at one end.
- **Do not add heavy material late** to a nearly full light load, which can tip it over the allowance.
- **Break down bulky items** so light debris packs efficiently and you are not tempted to compensate with heavy fill.

Most of all, match the box to the debris up front, so the allowance fits the load. Describe heavy or wet debris to the hauler before delivery.
