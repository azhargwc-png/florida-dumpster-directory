# Englewood Dumpster Permit and Placement Rules

Renting a dumpster in Englewood, FL means knowing who governs placement, where a box can sit, and where your debris goes. This guide covers the permit question, placement, and the local Charlotte and Sarasota County facilities.

## Who Governs Placement in Englewood

Englewood is an unincorporated coastal community that straddles the Charlotte, Sarasota county line, so the county that governs placement and facilities depends on where your property sits. A box on a public right-of-way follows the applicable county's rules; a box on your own driveway generally needs no permit.

## Placement and Local Factors

Englewood is swale country and split between two counties, so you must confirm which county your property is in, and keep a box off the swale (county right-of-way) unless you pull a permit. Mobile-home communities are also common.

**On your own driveway:** a box entirely on your private driveway usually needs no permit, which is the simplest option. Protect a paver or finished driveway with boards under the roll-off's contact points.

**On the street or right-of-way:** a box there follows the rules of whoever owns the road, so confirm the current requirement before placing one.

## Where Your Debris Goes

Englewood uses the facilities of whichever county your property is in. The West Charlotte Mini-Transfer and Recycling Facility at 7070 Environmental Way, Englewood serves the Charlotte-Englewood area for hazardous waste and self-haul; the Sarasota side uses the Chemical Collection Centers. Keep hazardous waste, sharps and appliances out of the dumpster, and confirm which county applies.
