# Dumpster for a Garage Cleanout

A garage cleanout is a classic dumpster job: a lot of bulky, mostly light debris, plus the hazardous items a garage tends to accumulate. This post covers it.

## Clearing the Garage

Garages collect boxes, old furniture, tools, sporting gear, broken items and general junk, most of it light and bulky, so a garage cleanout is volume-governed. Typically a 10- or 15-yard box fits a single or double garage; a packed, long-accumulated garage may need a 20-yard. The key extra step is sorting out the hazardous items a garage tends to hold: paint, solvents, motor oil, pesticides, pool chemicals, propane tanks and old batteries all go to the county facility, not the box. Tires go to a retailer or county facility. Set these aside first, then load the general junk. Donate usable tools and furniture. Describe the garage to the hauler for a size.
