# Dumpster Rental Checklist for Homeowners

This checklist is tailored to a homeowner renting a dumpster for a typical home project. It walks from booking to pickup.

## The Homeowner Checklist

For a home renovation, cleanout or project:

**Booking:** describe the project and debris to a local company for a size; ask what is included and the overage and extra-day rates.
**Placement:** choose a driveway spot (no permit); protect pavers with boards; clear the spot and overhead; mark the placement.
**Loading:** sort out banned items (hazardous waste, electronics, tires, refrigerant appliances) for the county facility; keep heavy debris in a small box; load efficiently; stay level with the fill line.
**Pickup:** confirm the box is level and accessible; call for pickup when done or extend before the period ends.

For a homeowner, the driveway-placement and banned-item steps are the ones most often overlooked, so pay attention to those. A local company handles the logistics.
