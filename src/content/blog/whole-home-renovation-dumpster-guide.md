# Whole-Home Renovation Dumpster Guide

A whole-home renovation produces debris across many rooms and phases, so it needs a large box, swaps, or multiple hauls. This post covers planning it.

## Whole-Home Renovation Debris

A whole-home renovation generates a large, sustained flow of mixed debris: drywall, flooring, cabinetry, fixtures, tile, and more, across rooms and over weeks. To handle it:

- **Size up and plan swaps.** A 20- or 30-yard box with scheduled swaps, or multiple hauls, suits most whole-home jobs.
- **Separate heavy material.** Tile, concrete and masonry go in a small box filled partway, apart from the general debris.
- **Time to the phases.** Demolition produces a burst; finishing produces a steady trickle. Match the box or swaps to the pace.
- **Mind Lee County's C and D recycling** requirement.
- **Route the exceptions:** hazardous materials, electronics, appliances to the county facility.

Describe the renovation scope, room count and timeline to the hauler, who plans the box and swaps across the project.
