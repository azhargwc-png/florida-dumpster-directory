# Dumpster for Tree and Brush Removal

Tree and brush removal produces bulky branch debris plus, often, heavy trunk sections. This post covers sizing and handling it.

## Tree and Brush Debris

Removing trees and brush produces:

- **Branches and brush:** bulky but light, filling volume. Cutting them down to size helps them pack.
- **Trunk sections and large limbs:** dense and heavy, especially green (freshly cut) wood, adding real weight.

So a brush-and-branch job sizes by volume (15- or 20-yard), but a job with large trunk sections adds weight, so tell the hauler. For a big tree removal, the trunk wood may warrant attention to the weight allowance. Note that county curbside programs collect routine and storm vegetative debris (within limits and, in Sarasota, under Leaf It Out), so a dumpster is for a large private removal or debris you cannot wait on. Describe the job to the hauler.
