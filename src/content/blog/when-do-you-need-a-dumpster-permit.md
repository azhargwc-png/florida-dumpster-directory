# When Do You Need a Dumpster Permit?

You generally need a permit only when the dumpster sits on public property, a street or right-of-way, not your own driveway. This post covers when a permit applies and who issues it.

## The Rule: Private vs Public Placement

The permit question comes down to where the box sits:

- **On your own driveway or lot (private property):** generally no permit needed.
- **On a public street or right-of-way:** generally a permit is required, from the city or county that owns the road.
- **On the swale:** in most Southwest Florida communities the swale fronting your lot is public right-of-way, not driveway, so a box there needs a permit too.

The permit is issued by whoever owns the road, your city in an incorporated area, or the county in an unincorporated one. In an HOA or deed-restricted community, the association may add its own placement rules on top. The simplest way to avoid a permit is to place the box on your own driveway. A local hauler will know the current requirement for your placement.
