# What Is a Dumpster Rental Deposit?

Some dumpster companies take a deposit at booking, others do not. Where one applies, it is usually a hold against overage, damage or prohibited items, refunded or applied to the final bill. This post explains how deposits work.

## How Deposits Work

A deposit, where a company uses one, is a sum collected at booking that acts as a hold. It typically covers the possibility of a weight overage, damage, or prohibited items in the load, and it is either refunded after pickup if none apply, or applied toward the final bill. Not every company requires a deposit, and the practice varies, so ask when you book whether there is one, what it covers, and how it is returned. Keeping your load under the weight allowance, free of banned items, and the box in good condition is what ensures a full refund where a deposit is held.
