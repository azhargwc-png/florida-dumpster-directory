# What Storm Debris the County Will and Will Not Take

County storm-debris collection takes eligible, separated debris set at the right-of-way, but not everything. Knowing what is in and out keeps your debris eligible and tells you what needs a dumpster or county facility. This post covers the line.

## In and Out of Curbside Collection

**What counties generally collect** (when separated and set at the right-of-way after a declared event):

- Vegetative/yard debris (branches, limbs, palm fronds).
- Construction and demolition debris from storm damage.
- Appliances and white goods (set out separately).
- Electronics (separate).

**What they will not take curbside:**

- Household hazardous waste (goes to the county facility).
- Debris in black bags (must be visible).
- Debris not separated into proper piles.
- Private-property debris you have not set at the right-of-way.
- Commercial debris (follows commercial rules).

What is out of curbside collection goes to a private dumpster (private-property, rebuild, commercial) or the county facility (hazardous waste, appliances via the proper route). Rules vary by county and event, so check your county's current guidance.
