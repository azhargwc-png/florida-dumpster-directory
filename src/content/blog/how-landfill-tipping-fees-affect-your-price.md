# How Landfill Tipping Fees Affect Your Price

Tipping fees are what the disposal facility charges the hauler to drop off debris, billed by weight. They are a major reason dumpster cost depends on weight, and understanding them explains the weight allowance and overage.

## What Tipping Fees Are

A tipping fee is the per-ton charge a landfill or transfer station bills the hauler each time they drop a load. Because it is charged by weight, a heavier load costs the hauler more to dispose of, and that cost flows into your rental as the included weight allowance and the per-ton overage above it. This is the mechanism behind weight-based pricing: your rental includes a tonnage allowance that reflects the tipping fee the hauler expects to pay, and exceeding it bills the extra per ton. It is also why dense debris (concrete, tile, dirt) costs more than bulky light debris of the same volume, and why clean recyclable loads can sometimes be disposed of more cheaply than mixed debris headed for the landfill.

## Get a Real Number for Your Job

Because the total depends on your specific debris, size, days and location, the only accurate figure is a quote from a local company for your job. Describe the project and they account for every driver and fee, so the number reflects what you will actually pay.
