# What Is a Right-of-Way Permit for a Dumpster?

A right-of-way permit authorizes placing a dumpster on public land, a street, right-of-way or swale. It is the permit most Florida homeowners encounter when the box cannot go on the driveway. This post explains it.

## What the Permit Covers

The public right-of-way is the strip of public land along a road, which in most Southwest Florida communities includes the swale fronting your lot, not just the paved street. A right-of-way permit authorizes temporarily placing something there, such as a dumpster. It is issued by the authority that owns the road, your city or the county, and typically:

- **Sets a time limit** for how long the box can stay.
- **Sets conditions:** not obstructing traffic, drainage, sightlines or utilities; sometimes requiring reflectors or markings.
- **Carries a fee.**

To get one, apply to the city or county (some haulers will do this for you and pass through the cost). The permit exists because the right-of-way is public and shared, so placing a heavy box there needs authorization. Keeping the box on your driveway avoids needing one.
