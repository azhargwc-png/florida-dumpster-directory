# Best Day to Schedule Dumpster Delivery

The best delivery day is the one that lines the box up with the start of your work. This post covers timing the delivery.

## Timing the Delivery

The goal is to have the box arrive when you are ready to load, so no rental days are wasted:

- **Deliver at the start of the work,** when debris begins, not days before.
- **For a demolition,** deliver just before or as the demo starts, so the box fills right away.
- **For a cleanout,** deliver once sorting is done or scheduled.
- **Allow for booking lead time,** a few days ahead for a standard job, more in busy periods or remote/island locations.
- **Avoid a Friday delivery if you want weekend work** but the company does not pick up on weekends, confirm pickup days.

So book ahead to secure the box, but schedule the actual delivery to the start of your loading. Describe your timeline to the hauler, who can suggest a delivery day that fits.
