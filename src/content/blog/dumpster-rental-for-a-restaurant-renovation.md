# Dumpster Rental for a Restaurant Renovation

A restaurant renovation produces kitchen equipment, fixtures, flooring and finishes, a mix of bulky and heavy debris. This post covers it.

## Restaurant Renovation Debris

A restaurant renovation produces a distinctive mix:

- **Kitchen equipment:** old appliances and stainless, heavy and often with refrigerant (coolers, walk-ins) needing recovery.
- **Fixtures and finishes:** booths, counters, flooring, tile, drywall, ceiling.
- **Heavy material:** tile, concrete, any masonry.

A 20- or 30-yard box with swaps handles the general debris, with heavy material (tile, concrete) in a separate small box, and refrigerant equipment routed for recovery. Confirm placement and any permit at the commercial site. Describe the renovation to the hauler, who sizes the boxes and plans swaps.
