# Venice Dumpster Permit and Placement Rules

Renting a dumpster in Venice, FL means knowing who governs placement, where a box can sit, and where your debris goes. This guide covers the permit question, placement, and the local Sarasota County facilities.

## Who Governs Placement in Venice

Venice is an incorporated city in Sarasota County, so the City of Venice governs its streets and rights-of-way, while Sarasota County runs the waste facilities.

## Placement and Local Factors

Venice's older coastal neighborhoods, the island section, and nearby retiree communities can mean tighter lots, so plan placement and truck access carefully. Venice is also home to the Sarasota County South Chemical Collection Center.

**On your own driveway:** a box entirely on your private driveway usually needs no permit, which is the simplest option. Protect a paver or finished driveway with boards under the roll-off's contact points.

**On the street or right-of-way:** a box there follows the rules of whoever owns the road, so confirm the current requirement before placing one.

## The Leaf It Out Yard-Waste Rule

Sarasota County's Leaf It Out rule bans plastic bags from yard-waste collection: yard waste must be in reusable containers or paper bags, within size limits, and bags brought to the landfill must be emptied. For a large landscaping tear-out beyond the curbside limits, a rented dumpster is the practical route.

## Where Your Debris Goes

Venice is home to the Sarasota County Chemical Collection Center (South) at 250 S. Jackson Road, which takes household hazardous waste and electronics free for residents year-round. Larger debris goes to the county landfill at 4000 Knights Trail Road, Nokomis, or via a dumpster hauler.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need a permit for a dumpster in Venice?**
A box entirely on your own driveway in Venice usually needs no permit, which is the simplest placement. Venice is an incorporated city in Sarasota County, so the City of Venice governs its streets and rights-of-way, while Sarasota County runs the waste facilities. So a box on a public street or right-of-way follows the rules of whoever owns the road, and may need a permit. Confirm the current requirement before placing a box on the street, and check any HOA or community rules that may apply to placement and duration.

**Is the Venice Chemical Collection Center nearby?**
Yes. Venice is home to the Sarasota County Chemical Collection Center (South) at 250 S. Jackson Road, which is convenient for Venice-area residents. It takes household hazardous waste and electronics free year-round, including paint, solvents, pesticides, pool chemicals and batteries. Pharmaceuticals are no longer accepted there. None of these items go in a dumpster, so set them aside before loading a roll-off for a Venice renovation or cleanout and use the South center.

**What size dumpster do I need in Venice?**
It depends on the job and the debris weight. A cleanout or shingle re-roof usually fits a 20-yard box, a bathroom remodel a 15-yard, and a whole-home renovation a 20- or 30-yard. Heavy debris like concrete or tile goes in a smaller 10-yard box filled partway, since weight hits the limit before the box looks full. Confirm the size by weight with a dumpster company serving Venice, and keep hazardous waste, electronics and appliances out of the box.

**Do dumpster companies serve Venice?**
Yes. Venice is served by dumpster rental companies covering the Sarasota County area.
