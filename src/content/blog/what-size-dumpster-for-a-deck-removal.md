# What Size Dumpster for a Deck Removal

Choosing a dumpster size for a deck removal comes down to the debris type and volume. A deck removal usually fits a 15- or 20-yard box. Deck boards are bulky, and the concrete footings add weight, so account for both.

## Sizing A deck removal

A typical deck removal fits a 15- or 20-yard box. The decking and framing (often pressure-treated lumber) are bulky and drive the volume, while the concrete post footings, if dug out, add weight. A small deck may fit a 10- or 15-yard; a large multi-level deck a 20-yard. Mention the footings to the hauler so the weight allowance accounts for them.

## Weight vs Volume

The reason sizing is not just about volume is that a dumpster also has a weight limit. Light, bulky debris fills the volume first; heavy material (concrete, tile, dirt, stone) reaches the weight limit first and goes in a smaller box filled partway. Matching the box to both factors is what avoids an overage charge or a second haul. See the [demolition dumpster page](/demolition-dumpster-rental/).

## Confirm With the Hauler

The surest way to size a deck removal is to describe it to the hauler: the scope, the rooms or square footage, and the kind of debris. The [size calculator](/tools/dumpster-size-calculator/) gives a starting estimate, and the hauler confirms the box and weight allowance from there, so you neither run out of room nor pay for space you do not use.
