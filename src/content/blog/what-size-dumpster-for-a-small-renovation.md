# What Size Dumpster for a Small Renovation

Choosing a dumpster size for a small renovation comes down to the debris type and volume. A small renovation usually fits a 10- or 15-yard box. For a single room with light debris, a 15-yard is the common choice.

## Sizing A small renovation

A small renovation, one room or a minor project, usually fits a 10- or 15-yard box. A 15-yard suits a single-room remodel with general light-to-moderate debris, while a 10-yard fits a smaller job or one with heavy material like tile. Describe the room and debris type to the hauler. For a project spanning several rooms, step up to a 20-yard.

## Weight vs Volume

The reason sizing is not just about volume is that a dumpster also has a weight limit. Light, bulky debris fills the volume first; heavy material (concrete, tile, dirt, stone) reaches the weight limit first and goes in a smaller box filled partway. Matching the box to both factors is what avoids an overage charge or a second haul. See the [15-yard dumpster page](/15-yard-dumpster-rental/).

## Confirm With the Hauler

The surest way to size a small renovation is to describe it to the hauler: the scope, the rooms or square footage, and the kind of debris. The [size calculator](/tools/dumpster-size-calculator/) gives a starting estimate, and the hauler confirms the box and weight allowance from there, so you neither run out of room nor pay for space you do not use.
