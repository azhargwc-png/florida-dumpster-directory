# Will a Dumpster Crack My Driveway?

A loaded dumpster is heavy, so driveway damage is a real but preventable risk. The risk and the protection vary by surface. This post covers it.

## The Risk by Surface

A loaded roll-off weighs several tons on its contact points, and the delivery truck adds more, so without protection a driveway can crack, crush or stain. The risk by surface:

- **Pavers:** highest risk, individual units can crack or shift. Boards essential.
- **Stamped or decorative concrete:** can crack or mar the finish. Boards advised.
- **Sealed concrete:** can stain or scuff. Boards advised.
- **Plain concrete:** most forgiving, but extreme point loads can still crack and the surface can mark. Boards are cheap insurance.
- **Asphalt:** can dent or depress, especially in Florida heat when asphalt softens. Boards advised.

Across all surfaces, boards (planks or plywood) under the box's rails and the truck's path spread the load and prevent most damage. So a dumpster will not crack your driveway if you protect it, which is simple and cheap relative to a repair. Have boards down before delivery.
