# What Is a Trip or Dry-Run Fee?

A trip fee (or dry-run fee) is charged when the truck makes a scheduled run to your site but cannot complete it. It is entirely avoidable with a little preparation. This post explains when it applies and how to prevent it.

## When a Trip Fee Applies

A trip fee applies when the hauler dispatches a truck to deliver or collect the box but cannot do the work, for example:

- The placement spot is blocked by a vehicle, gate or obstruction.
- Access is too tight or soft for the truck to reach.
- The box is overfilled above the fill line and cannot be legally hauled.
- Nobody is available to grant access where that is required.

The truck made the trip, so the fee covers the wasted run. Avoid it by keeping the delivery and pickup spot clear and accessible, confirming the truck's approach when you book, and keeping the load level with the fill line so it is ready for collection.
