# How to Load a Dumpster Safely

Safe loading prevents the most common dumpster injuries. This post covers how to load safely.

## Safe Loading Technique

To load without injury:

- **Lift properly.** Bend your knees, keep your back straight, hold the load close, and get help or use a dolly for heavy items.
- **Work from outside the box.** Do not climb in; load from the open side or the door.
- **Break items down outside** so they pack without stomping inside.
- **Distribute weight evenly** across the floor for safe truck lifting.
- **Handle sharp debris carefully.** Gloves, sturdy footwear, and care with nails, glass and metal edges.
- **Do not overreach** tossing items up into a high box, move closer or use the door.
- **Keep the load level** with the fill line.

Most injuries come from lifting wrong, climbing in, or sharp debris, so careful lifting, staying outside, and protective gear prevent them. Describe your debris to the hauler for a box that loads safely (a low box for heavy material).
