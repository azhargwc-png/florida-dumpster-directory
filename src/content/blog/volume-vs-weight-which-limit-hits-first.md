# Volume vs Weight: Which Limit Hits First

Every dumpster load hits either its volume limit or its weight limit first, and which one tells you how to size the box. This post explains how to tell.

## Which Limit Governs

A dumpster has two limits, volume (how much fits) and weight (how heavy it can be), and your load hits one of them first:

- **Volume governs** when the debris is light and bulky: you fill the box with space to spare on weight. Size by the yards, and use the full box. Examples: a furniture cleanout, a wood-framing demo, a packaging clear-out.
- **Weight governs** when the debris is heavy and dense: you hit the weight allowance while the box looks partly full. Size by weight, use a small box filled partway. Examples: concrete, tile, dirt.

Knowing which governs tells you how to size and load: for a volume-governed job, fill the box; for a weight-governed job, stop at the allowance. A mixed load can hit weight first because of a heavy fraction, so separate heavy material. Describe the debris to the hauler, who identifies the governing limit and sizes accordingly.
