# Managing Roofing Debris on Your Property

A re-roof drops a lot of debris fast, and managing it protects your property and keeps the load clean. This post covers roofing debris management.

## Managing the Tear-Off

During a re-roof, old roofing comes off fast and in quantity, so managing it matters:

- **Position the box close to the roof** so debris can be loaded directly, reducing yard mess.
- **Protect the yard and landscaping** under the eaves with tarps to catch debris and nails.
- **Keep the load clean** (just roofing) for weight accuracy and recycling.
- **Mind the weight**, especially for tile, filling to the allowance, not the rim.
- **Watch for nails** in the yard and driveway; a magnetic sweep after helps.
- **Protect the driveway** under the box with boards.

Most re-roofs are done by a roofing crew who manage this, with the dumpster as their disposal point; for a DIY tear-off, these steps keep your property clean and the load within weight. Describe the roof to the hauler for the right box.
