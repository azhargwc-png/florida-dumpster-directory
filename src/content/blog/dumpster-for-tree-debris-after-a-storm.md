# Dumpster for Tree Debris After a Storm

Storms bring down trees and limbs, generating heavy vegetative debris. County programs usually collect eligible yard debris, but a private dumpster handles large private-property tree removal. This post covers the options.

## Tree Debris: County vs Dumpster

Fallen trees, large limbs and brush are among the most common storm debris. Your options:

- **County curbside collection.** After a declared storm, counties collect eligible vegetative debris set at the right-of-way, separated from other debris (not bagged, clear of utilities). This is the usual route for yard debris and does not require a rental.
- **A private dumpster.** For a large private-property tree removal, debris you are clearing from inside your lot, or debris you cannot wait on, a roll-off handles it. Tree debris is bulky but can be heavy when green and dense, especially with trunk sections, so tell the hauler.

Note the Leaf It Out rule in Sarasota County (no plastic bags for yard waste). Keep the vegetative debris separate from construction debris so each goes to the right place.
