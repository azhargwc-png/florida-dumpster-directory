# Dumpster for a Home Addition Build

A home addition generates two kinds of debris: demolition of the existing structure where the addition ties in, and construction offcuts from the new build. This post covers handling both.

## Addition Debris: Demo Plus Build

A home addition produces debris in two phases:

- **Demolition debris** from opening up the existing structure, removing a wall, roof section or exterior, where the addition connects. This can include drywall, framing, roofing, siding, and sometimes masonry.
- **Construction debris** from the new build: lumber offcuts, packaging, drywall scrap, flooring and roofing waste.

For most additions, a 20-yard box handles the general debris of both phases, with swaps if the job runs long, and a separate small box for any concrete or masonry (from footings or an opened block wall). Keep hazardous items out. In Lee County, the C and D recycling requirement applies. Describe the addition scope and whether it involves concrete to the hauler, who will size the box and plan swaps across the project's phases.
