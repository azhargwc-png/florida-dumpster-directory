# What Is a Roll-Off Dumpster?

A roll-off dumpster is the open-top steel container delivered and collected by a truck that rolls it on and off its bed. It is the standard container for renovation, cleanout and construction debris. This post explains what it is.

## What Makes It a Roll-Off

A roll-off is a rectangular, open-top steel container that a special truck delivers by tilting its bed and rolling the box off on wheels, then collects the same way, hence 'roll-off.' Key features:

- **Open top,** so you can load from above or, on many boxes, through a door at one end.
- **Sizes from 10 to 40 cubic yards,** chosen by the job.
- **Delivered and hauled by one truck,** on a set rental period.

It differs from the permanent bins behind businesses (front-load dumpsters emptied in place) and from a dumpster bag (a small fabric bag). The roll-off is the temporary, project-sized container you rent for a renovation, cleanout, roof or construction job, and it is what this directory's companies provide.
