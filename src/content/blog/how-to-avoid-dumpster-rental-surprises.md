# How to Avoid Dumpster Rental Surprises

Most dumpster rental surprises come from not asking a few questions up front. This post covers how to avoid them.

## Heading Off Surprises

To avoid surprises:

- **Ask what is included** (days, weight allowance) and the overage and extra-day rates, so the bill holds no surprises.
- **Describe your debris accurately,** especially heavy or banned items, so the size and quote fit.
- **Confirm placement and any permit** before delivery, so there is no fine or trip fee.
- **Know what cannot go in** the box, so there is no contamination charge.
- **Keep the load level with the fill line,** so there is no overfill problem.
- **Confirm access** for the truck, so delivery and pickup are not wasted trips.

Almost every surprise, a fee, a wrong size, a failed delivery, traces to a question not asked or a detail not described. A short conversation with the hauler up front heads them off.
