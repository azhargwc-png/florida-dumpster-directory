# What to Do When the Dumpster Is Full

When the box is full, a couple of simple steps get it collected or get you more capacity. This post covers what to do.

## When the Box Is Full

When the dumpster is full (level with the fill line):

- **If the job is done:** call the company to schedule pickup. Make sure the load is level with the fill line (not heaped above) and the box is accessible, so the pickup is not a wasted trip.
- **If you have more debris:** arrange a swap (the hauler collects the full box and drops an empty one on the same spot) or a second box, rather than overfilling.
- **Do not overfill.** A load heaped above the fill line cannot be legally hauled or tarped, so it causes a refused pickup or an overfill fee.
- **Check the weight** if the box is heavy but not visually full, you may be at the weight limit even with space left.

So a full box means either pickup (job done) or a swap/second box (more to come), never overfilling. Call the hauler for either.
