# Dumpster Rental Checklist for Contractors

This checklist is tailored to a contractor managing job-site debris with dumpsters. It covers planning, swaps and compliance.

## The Contractor Checklist

For a job site:

**Planning:** size boxes to the project pace; plan swaps for a long job; separate heavy material (concrete, masonry) into a small box.
**Placement:** position for crew and truck access; protect the surface; confirm any permit for street placement.
**Compliance:** keep hazardous and restricted items out; in Lee County, route C and D debris through the mandatory recycling; keep clean loads where recycling matters.
**Operation:** keep the box on site so crews deposit debris rather than stockpiling; schedule swaps before the box overfills; keep loads within weight allowances.
**Cost control:** know the overage and extra-day rates; match the box and swaps to the debris flow.

For contractors, the key extras over a homeowner are swaps, heavy-material separation, and Lee County's recycling requirement.
