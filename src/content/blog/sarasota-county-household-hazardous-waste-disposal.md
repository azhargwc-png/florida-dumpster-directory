# Sarasota County Household Hazardous Waste Disposal

Household hazardous waste cannot go in a roll-off dumpster or the regular trash, and Sarasota County accepts it free year-round at two Chemical Collection Centers. This post covers what counts, where it goes, what the centers no longer take, the Re-Uz-It Shop, and how to separate everything before a dumpster job.

## What Counts as Household Hazardous Waste

Most household hazardous waste cannot go curbside. (cite index="122-1">Due to safety and cost, most household hazardous wastes are not collected curbside; residents may take accepted items generated from non-business activities to a Chemical Collection Center for safe disposal free of charge.</cite> In a typical home that means paint and solvents, pesticides, pool chemicals, automotive products, antifreeze, aerosols and batteries. County collection-event lists name items like adhesives, aerosols, antifreeze, automotive products, pesticides, pool chemicals, propane cylinders, rechargeable and vehicle batteries, and solvents.

## Where It Goes: The Chemical Collection Centers

Sarasota County runs two year-round centers, open to all residents. (cite index="124-1">Hazardous materials and electronics may be taken year-round to the Chemical Collection Center (North) at 8750 Bee Ridge Road, Sarasota, and the Chemical Collection Center (South) at 250 S. Jackson Road, Venice.</cite> (cite index="114-1">All residents in Sarasota County may use the household hazardous waste disposal services.</cite>

The program's goal is reuse and safe processing, not just disposal. (cite index="117-1">The purpose of the household hazardous waste programs is to provide a safe solution for household hazardous waste processing rather than disposal.</cite> Usable items are resold cheaply. (cite index="127-1">Items brought to the Chemical Collection Centers that are still in usable condition make up the inventory found in The Re-Uz-It Shop.</cite>

## What the Centers No Longer Take

One change to note: (cite index="122-1">Pharmaceuticals are no longer accepted at the Sarasota County Chemical Collection Centers, and multiple drop-off locations for prescription drugs are available throughout Sarasota County.</cite> So route unwanted medications to a pharmacy or sheriff's office drop-off, not a Chemical Collection Center.

## Keeping It Out of Your Dumpster

When you rent a roll-off for a renovation or cleanout, pull the hazardous items first, since one banned item can trigger a contamination charge. Before loading:

- Set aside paint, solvents, automotive products, antifreeze, pesticides and pool chemicals for a Chemical Collection Center.
- Separate electronics for the same centers.
- Keep rechargeable and vehicle batteries and propane cylinders out of the box.
- Flag any refrigerant appliance to the hauler.

One trip to a Chemical Collection Center before you load keeps the dumpster load clean and compliant with Florida Statute 403.708, which restricts lead-acid batteries, used oil and large appliances from standard landfill disposal.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Where do I take household hazardous waste in Sarasota County?**
To one of two Chemical Collection Centers, free for all Sarasota County residents: the North center at 8750 Bee Ridge Road, Sarasota, or the South center at 250 S. Jackson Road, Venice. Both accept hazardous materials and electronics year-round, including paint, solvents, pesticides, pool chemicals, automotive products, antifreeze, aerosols and batteries. Pharmaceuticals are no longer accepted there. None of these items go in a dumpster, so separate them before loading a roll-off for a renovation or cleanout.

**Are pharmaceuticals accepted at the Sarasota County centers?**
No. Pharmaceuticals are no longer accepted at the Sarasota County Chemical Collection Centers. The county has multiple drop-off locations for prescription drugs available throughout Sarasota County instead, often at pharmacies and sheriff's office locations. So route unwanted or expired medications to one of those, not a Chemical Collection Center and never a dumpster. The Chemical Collection Centers still take the full range of household hazardous waste and electronics free for residents.

**What is the Re-Uz-It Shop?**
The Re-Uz-It Shop is Sarasota County's reuse program. Items brought to the Chemical Collection Centers that are still in usable condition become its inventory, so usable paint, chemicals and similar products get a second life rather than being disposed of. It reflects the program's purpose, which is safe processing and reuse rather than straight disposal. If you are starting a project, it can be worth checking the Re-Uz-It Shop for usable products before buying new.

**Does household hazardous waste go in a dumpster in Sarasota County?**
No. Paint, solvents, automotive products, antifreeze, pesticides, pool chemicals, aerosols, batteries and propane cylinders cannot go in a roll-off dumpster, because they contaminate the load and are dangerous at the landfill. In Sarasota County they go free to a Chemical Collection Center in Sarasota or Venice. Putting a hazardous item in a dumpster can trigger a contamination charge or a rejected load, so set these aside before loading and make one trip to a Chemical Collection Center.
