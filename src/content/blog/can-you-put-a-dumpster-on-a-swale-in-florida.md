# Can You Put a Dumpster on a Swale in Florida?

A swale looks like part of your yard, but in most Southwest Florida communities it is public right-of-way, so placing a dumpster there needs a permit and risks drainage damage. This post explains the swale rule.

## The Swale Is Public Right-of-Way

The swale is the shallow drainage channel between your lot and the road, part of the community's stormwater system. Although it fronts your property, in most Southwest Florida communities it is public right-of-way, not an extension of your driveway. That means:

- **A dumpster on the swale needs a right-of-way permit** from the city or county.
- **A heavy box can damage the swale,** crushing or rutting the graded channel and impeding drainage, which you could be responsible for repairing.
- **Placing one without a permit can draw a fine.**

The simplest approach is to keep the box on your own driveway, which is private property and generally needs no permit. If you must use the swale, pull the permit and consider boards to spread the load. A local hauler will know the swale rule for your community.
