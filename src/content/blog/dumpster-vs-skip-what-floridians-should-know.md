# Dumpster vs Skip: What Floridians Should Know

Visitors and transplants sometimes ask for a skip. In the US, the equivalent is a roll-off dumpster. This post clears up the terms.

## Skip vs Dumpster: Same Idea, Different Term

A 'skip' is the British and Commonwealth term for what Americans call a roll-off dumpster, an open-top container delivered and collected for a project. So if you know the term skip (from the UK, Ireland, Australia and elsewhere), a roll-off dumpster is the Florida equivalent. The concept is the same: a container rented for a project, filled, and hauled away. The differences are mostly terminology and the delivery method, US roll-offs are rolled off a tilting truck bed, and sizing is in cubic yards rather than the skip's typical cubic-yard or 'builder's skip' sizing. So ask a Florida company for a roll-off dumpster, describe your job, and you will get the skip equivalent sized to the work.
