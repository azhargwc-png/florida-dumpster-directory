# Dumpster Dimensions: Length, Width and Height by Size

This is a quick reference for common roll-off dumpster dimensions in Southwest Florida. Exact sizes vary by hauler, so treat these as typical ranges and confirm with your company when you book.

## Typical Dimensions by Size

| Size | Typical length | Width | Height | Holds about |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10-yard | 12-14 ft | 8 ft | 3.5-4 ft | 3-4 pickup loads |
| 15-yard | 14-16 ft | 8 ft | 4-4.5 ft | 4-5 pickup loads |
| 20-yard | 20-22 ft | 8 ft | 4-4.5 ft | 6-8 pickup loads |
| 30-yard | 20-22 ft | 8 ft | 6 ft | 9-12 pickup loads |
| 40-yard | 22 ft | 8 ft | 8 ft | 12-16 pickup loads |

## What the Numbers Mean

Notice two patterns. First, the 20-yard and 30-yard share a footprint (around 20-22 feet by 8 feet); the 30-yard just has taller walls, so it holds more without needing more ground space. The same is true stepping up to the 40-yard. Second, the lower boxes (10- and 15-yard) have low walls that are easy to load by hand, which suits the heavy debris they often carry, while the taller boxes suit bulky, light debris tossed up higher.

## Footprint and Placement

All common sizes are about 8 feet wide, so they fit a standard driveway, but the length matters for placement and the clearance the delivery truck needs. A 10-yard needs the least room; a 30- or 40-yard needs a longer, clear space. Confirm the length against your driveway or placement spot before booking.

## Weight, Not Just Size

Dimensions tell you volume, not weight. Every box also has a weight limit, and heavy debris reaches it before the box fills, which is why concrete and dirt go in a small box filled partway regardless of dimensions. Match the box to both the volume and the weight of your debris.
