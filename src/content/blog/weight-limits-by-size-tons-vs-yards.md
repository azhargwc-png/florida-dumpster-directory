# Weight Limits by Size: Tons vs Yards

A dumpster is measured in cubic yards (volume) but also has a weight limit in tons, and the two are different constraints. Understanding both is the key to sizing and to avoiding an overage. This is the weight-cluster pillar.

## Yards vs Tons

**Yards (volume)** is the box's capacity, how much space the debris fills. Sizes run 10 to 40 yards.

**Tons (weight)** is how heavy the load can be, set by what the truck can lift and what the disposal facility charges for. Each rental includes a weight allowance in tons.

The two interact: light, bulky debris fills the volume first (you run out of space before weight), while heavy, dense debris hits the weight limit first (the box is heavy before it looks full). So:

- **Light debris** (furniture, wood, packaging): size by volume, use the yards.
- **Heavy debris** (concrete, tile, dirt): size by weight, use a small box filled partway.

Going over the weight allowance triggers a per-ton overage, regardless of how much volume is left. Matching the box to both constraints, and describing heavy debris to the hauler, is how you size correctly and avoid an overage.
