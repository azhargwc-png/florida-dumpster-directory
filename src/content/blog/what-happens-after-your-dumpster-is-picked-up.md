# What Happens After Your Dumpster Is Picked Up

After pickup, the box goes to a disposal facility where the load is weighed, emptied, and sorted for disposal or recycling. This post follows the debris after it leaves your property.

## Where the Debris Goes

Once the truck collects your box, it hauls it to a landfill or transfer station. There:

- **The load is weighed,** which determines any weight overage on your bill.
- **The box is emptied.**
- **The debris is handled** by type: general debris to disposal, and recyclable material diverted where the facility and hauler support it. Clean concrete can be crushed into aggregate, clean metal and wood diverted, and in Lee County, mandatory C and D recycling means construction debris is routed to a recycling-capable facility.

The empty box is then returned to service for the next rental. For you, the practical result is the final weight-based cost (set by the weighing) and the knowledge that recyclable material is diverted where possible, especially from a clean load.
