# Dumpster Rental vs Hauling It Yourself

For a cleanout or project, you can rent a dumpster or haul the debris yourself in a truck or trailer. This post weighs the trade-off.

## Rent vs Haul Yourself

**Hauling it yourself** (with a truck or trailer) can be cheaper for a small amount if you have the vehicle, but you pay disposal fees at the facility, make multiple trips for any volume, do all the loading and unloading, and spend the time. It suits a few items.

**Renting a dumpster** costs more upfront but handles a large volume in one container on site, with no trips, no unloading, and disposal included within the allowance. It suits a cleanout or project.

So for a few items and a spare truck, hauling yourself can win; for any real volume, a dumpster saves the trips, the labor and the time. Factor in the facility's disposal fees and the value of your time. Describe a larger job to the hauler for a dumpster size.
