# What a Dumpster Rental Quote Should Include

A complete dumpster quote spells out more than one number. Knowing what it should include lets you compare companies fairly and avoid surprises. This post lists what to confirm before you book.

## What to Confirm in a Quote

A complete quote should make clear:

- **The box size** and roughly what it holds.
- **What the base price includes:** delivery, the rental period, pickup, haul and disposal.
- **The weight allowance** (in tons) and the per-ton overage rate above it.
- **The rental period** (days included) and the extra-day rate.
- **Whether a permit is needed** for your placement, and who arranges it.
- **Any special-item surcharges** (mattresses, appliances, tires).
- **Delivery logistics:** access needs and any trip-fee conditions.

With those spelled out, you can compare two companies on the same basis rather than on headline numbers, and you will know what could add to the total.

## Get a Real Number for Your Job

Because the total depends on your specific debris, size, days and location, the only accurate figure is a quote from a local company for your job. Describe the project and they account for every driver and fee, so the number reflects what you will actually pay.
