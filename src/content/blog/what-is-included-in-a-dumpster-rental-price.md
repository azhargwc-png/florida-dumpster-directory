# What Is Included in a Dumpster Rental Price?

A dumpster rental price usually bundles several things: delivery, the rental period, pickup, haul and disposal, and a weight allowance. Knowing what is included helps you compare quotes and see what might cost extra.

## What the Base Price Usually Covers

A typical rental price includes:

- **Delivery** of the box to your site.
- **The rental period** (a set number of days).
- **Pickup and haul** at the end.
- **Disposal** of the debris (within the weight allowance).
- **A weight allowance** (a number of tons).

What may cost extra:

- **Weight over the allowance** (per-ton overage).
- **Extra days** beyond the included period.
- **A permit** for street or right-of-way placement.
- **Special items** some haulers surcharge (such as mattresses or appliances).
- **A trip fee** if the truck cannot deliver or collect on arrival.

Asking what is included and what the extras are is how you compare quotes fairly.

## Get a Real Number for Your Job

Because the total depends on your specific debris, size, days and location, the only accurate figure is a quote from a local company for your job. Describe the project, the debris, scope and location, and they account for every driver and fee so the number reflects what you will actually pay.
