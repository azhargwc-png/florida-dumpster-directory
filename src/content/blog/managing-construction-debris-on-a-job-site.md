# Managing Construction Debris on a Job Site

Good debris management keeps a job site clear, safe and moving. A dumpster (with swaps) is the backbone. This post covers managing site debris.

## Keeping the Site Clear

On an active job site, debris builds up fast, and managing it well matters for safety, efficiency and compliance:

- **Keep a box on site** so crews deposit debris as they go rather than stockpiling it, which keeps walkways and work areas clear.
- **Schedule swaps** on a longer job so an empty box is always available.
- **Separate heavy material** (concrete, masonry) into a dedicated small box for weight and recycling.
- **Keep restricted items out** (hazardous waste, and in some cases appliances and electronics), routing them properly.
- **Mind Lee County's C and D recycling** requirement, which the hauler routes accordingly.
- **Position the box for access** by both crews and the roll-off truck.

Describe the project pace and debris mix to the hauler so they size the box and plan swaps to keep the site clear throughout.
