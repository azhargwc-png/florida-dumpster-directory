# Garage Conversion Dumpster Guide

Converting a garage into living space produces demolition debris (the garage interior) plus build-out debris. This post covers handling it. (Basements are rare in Southwest Florida, so this focuses on garage conversions.)

## Garage Conversion Debris

A garage conversion, turning a garage into a room, suite or ADU, is common in Southwest Florida (basements are rare here). It produces:

- **Demolition debris:** old garage door, any existing interior, and material removed to frame walls, add insulation and finish.
- **Build-out debris:** drywall offcuts, flooring and fixture packaging, lumber scrap.
- **Possibly concrete:** if the slab is cut for plumbing or leveled.

A 15- or 20-yard box handles the general debris of most garage conversions; any concrete from slab work goes in a separate small box. Route hazardous items and the old garage-door opener/electronics appropriately. Describe the conversion scope, and whether it involves concrete slab work, to the hauler for a size.
