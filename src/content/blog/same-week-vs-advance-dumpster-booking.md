# Same-Week vs Advance Dumpster Booking

You can often book a dumpster for same-week delivery, but advance booking is safer in some situations. This post covers when to do which.

## Same-Week vs Advance

**Same-week booking** often works for a standard job outside peak demand, companies can frequently deliver within a few days or even next-day. Fine when your timeline is flexible and demand is normal.

**Advance booking** is safer when: demand is high (busy season, after a storm), the location is remote or an island (tighter scheduling), you need a specific delivery day, or the job is large or multi-box. Booking ahead secures the box and the day you want.

So for a flexible standard job in normal conditions, same-week is usually fine; for a fixed date, a busy period, a remote location, or a big job, book in advance. Either way, time the delivery to the start of your work. Describe your timeline and location to the hauler.
