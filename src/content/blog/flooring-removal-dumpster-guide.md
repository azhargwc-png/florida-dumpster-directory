# Flooring Removal Dumpster Guide

Flooring removal sizing depends entirely on the material: tile is heavy, wood and vinyl are light and bulky. This post covers handling a floor tear-out.

## Flooring Removal by Material

A floor tear-out produces the flooring plus underlayment, and the material decides everything:

- **Tile and stone:** heavy, plus thinset mortar. A large tile floor leans to a smaller box with attention to weight.
- **Wood, laminate, vinyl:** light and bulky; a whole-house tear-out sizes by volume (15- or 20-yard).
- **Carpet:** rolls in; wet carpet is heavy.
- **Watch old vinyl tile** in pre-1980s homes for asbestos, test before removal.

See [what size dumpster for flooring removal](/blog/what-size-dumpster-for-flooring-removal/). Tell the hauler the flooring type and the area, since the material drives whether weight or volume governs.
