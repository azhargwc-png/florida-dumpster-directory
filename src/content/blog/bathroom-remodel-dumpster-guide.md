# Bathroom Remodel Dumpster Guide

A bathroom remodel is small in area but can be heavy, since tile and fixtures add up. This pillar guide covers sizing and handling bathroom debris.

## Bathroom Remodel Debris

A bathroom gut produces tile, a tub or shower, a toilet and sink, vanity and cabinetry, flooring, drywall and fixtures. It is a small space but can be heavy:

- **Size:** a 10- or 15-yard box fits most single bathrooms; a larger or multi-bathroom remodel a 20-yard.
- **Watch the weight.** Tile, thinset, and a cast-iron tub are heavy, so if there is a lot of tile, lean toward the smaller box and mind the weight allowance.
- **Empty fixtures** (drain the toilet) before they go in.
- **Route the exceptions:** old vanity-stored chemicals to the county facility; a cast-iron tub is heavy, so flag it.

See [what size dumpster for a bathroom remodel](/blog/what-size-dumpster-for-a-bathroom-remodel/). Describe the bathroom and whether it has heavy tile to the hauler.
