# Dumpster Rental in a Mobile-Home Community

Mobile-home and manufactured-home communities, common in Southwest Florida, have park rules that govern dumpster placement. This post covers it.

## Dumpsters in a Mobile-Home Park

In a mobile-home or manufactured-home community, the park or community owner controls placement, so a dumpster rental needs coordination:

- **Clear placement with management.** The park owner or management decides where (and whether) a box can sit, since lots are often rented and space is tight.
- **Mind tight lots and access.** Mobile-home lots and streets can be narrow, so a smaller box and careful truck access help.
- **Check park rules** on duration and screening.
- **Confirm the truck can reach** the lot.

So in a mobile-home community, clear the placement with management before booking, and give the hauler the access details. Describe the community and lot to the hauler, who can advise on a size that fits the space.
