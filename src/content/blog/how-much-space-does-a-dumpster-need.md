# How Much Space Does a Dumpster Need?

A dumpster needs space for the box itself plus room for the truck to deliver and collect it. This post covers both so you can judge whether your spot works.

## Space for the Box and the Truck

Two space needs:

**The box footprint.** All common sizes are about 8 feet wide. Length ranges from about 12-14 feet for a 10-yard to 20-22 feet for a 20- or 30-yard. Height ranges from about 3.5 feet (10-yard) to 8 feet (40-yard). So the box itself needs roughly a parking-space-plus footprint for a small box, more for a large one.

**The truck's working room.** The roll-off truck needs a straight approach to back in, clearance to one side, and overhead clearance (no low branches or wires) to tilt its bed high. It also needs room behind the placement spot to position. This is often the bigger constraint than the box footprint.

So judge your spot by both: can the box fit, and can the truck reach and work the spot? A smaller box eases both. Describe the spot to the hauler if unsure.
