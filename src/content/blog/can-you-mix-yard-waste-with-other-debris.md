# Can You Mix Yard Waste With Other Debris?

You can mix yard waste with other debris in a general dumpster, but keeping it separate sometimes helps with recycling and weight. This post covers when to mix and when to separate.

## Mixing vs Separating Yard Waste

Yard waste (branches, brush, plant material) can go in a general debris dumpster with construction or household debris, and for a small amount that is fine. When to keep it separate:

- **For recycling.** A clean yard-waste load can be mulched or composted, while mixed with construction debris it heads to disposal. If you have a lot of clean vegetation, a separate load preserves that.
- **For a big landscaping job.** Keep heavy sod and soil separate from both plant material and construction debris for weight.
- **County curbside** only takes separated yard waste, so if you are using curbside for some of it, keep it unmixed.

So mix a small amount of yard waste into a general load freely, but for a large clean-vegetation job, a separate load helps recycling. Describe the debris to the hauler.
