# Dumpster Rental Tips for First-Timers

Renting a dumpster for the first time is simple once you know the basics. This post is the first-timer's guide.

## First-Timer Basics

If this is your first dumpster rental, the essentials:

- **Describe your job to the hauler** for the right size, they do this all the time.
- **Driveway placement is simplest** (no permit); protect pavers with boards.
- **Heavy debris goes in a small box filled partway;** light debris sizes by volume.
- **Keep banned items out:** hazardous waste, tires, electronics, appliances with refrigerant, liquids, to the county facility.
- **Keep the load level with the fill line,** not heaped above.
- **Time the box to your work** so it is not sitting idle.
- **Ask what is included** (days, weight allowance) and the overage and extra-day rates.

That covers most of it. A local company walks you through the rest; the main thing you provide is an accurate description of the job.
