# How Long Is a Standard Dumpster Rental?

A standard dumpster rental runs for an included period, often around a week, with extra days available. This post covers the standard period.

## The Standard Period

A standard rental includes a set number of days in the base price, commonly in the range of about 7 to 10 days, though it varies by company. Within that period you load the box at your pace; each day beyond adds a daily rate. Some companies offer shorter or longer options. To use the period well, time delivery to your work, load as you go, and call for pickup when done or extend before the period ends. Confirm the included days and the extra-day rate when you book.
