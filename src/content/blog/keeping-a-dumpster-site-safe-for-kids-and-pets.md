# Keeping a Dumpster Site Safe for Kids and Pets

A dumpster and its debris are a hazard to curious kids and pets, so a few precautions keep them safe. This post covers it.

## Protecting Kids and Pets

A dumpster on site attracts curious children and pets, and the hazards, sharp debris, climbing, falling items, are real. To keep them safe:

- **Keep kids and pets away** from the box and the active work area, supervised or barriered.
- **Do not let children climb** on or into the box; debris, sharp edges and shifting loads are dangerous.
- **Sweep for nails and sharp debris** around the box, especially after roofing or demolition, since bare feet and paws are at risk.
- **Keep the load level** so items do not fall out onto someone.
- **Secure hazardous or small items** that a child or pet could reach.
- **Watch during delivery and pickup,** keeping everyone clear of the truck.

Treating the box and its surroundings as off-limits to kids and pets, and keeping the area swept and the load contained, prevents the injuries curious children and animals are most at risk of.
