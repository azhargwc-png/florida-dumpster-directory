# Flat Rate vs Variable Dumpster Pricing

Dumpster companies price in one of two ways: a flat rate that bundles a set size, weight and period, or variable pricing that charges for the base plus what you use. Knowing which you are getting helps you compare quotes and avoid surprises.

## Flat Rate vs Variable

**Flat rate** bundles a specific box size, a weight allowance, and a rental period into one price. It is predictable as long as you stay within those limits; exceeding the weight allowance or keeping the box longer adds to it. Good for a well-defined job.

**Variable pricing** charges a base (delivery, haul, a starting allowance) and then bills for actual tonnage and extra days. It can be cheaper for a light, quick job and costlier for a heavy or slow one. Good when the debris is uncertain.

Neither is inherently cheaper; they fit different jobs. Ask which structure a quote uses and what the overage and extra-day rates are.

## Get a Real Number for Your Job

Because the total depends on your specific debris, size, days and location, the only accurate figure is a quote from a local company for your job. Describe the project, the debris, scope and location, and they account for every driver and fee so the number reflects what you will actually pay.
