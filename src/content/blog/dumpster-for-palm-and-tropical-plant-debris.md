# Dumpster for Palm and Tropical Plant Debris

Palm fronds and tropical plant debris are distinctive Southwest Florida yard waste, bulky and awkward to pack. This post covers handling them.

## Palm and Tropical Debris

Southwest Florida's palms and tropical plants produce distinctive yard waste:

- **Palm fronds** are long, bulky and awkward, so they fan out and waste box space unless cut down. Cut them into shorter lengths to pack.
- **Palm trunks** are dense and heavy, especially when green, so a removed palm trunk adds real weight, like a tree trunk.
- **Tropical plants and shrubs** (bird of paradise, sea grape, etc.) are bulky and light.

So a palm-and-tropical clearing sizes by volume (15- or 20-yard), cut down to pack, with attention to weight if you are removing palm trunks. County curbside collects routine palm fronds within limits (and, in Sarasota, under Leaf It Out), so a dumpster is for a large clearing or trunk removal. Describe the job to the hauler.
