# Dumpster Rental for Property Managers

Property managers use dumpsters for unit turnovers, cleanouts and renovations, often across multiple units. This post covers it.

## Dumpsters for Property Management

A property manager's dumpster needs span:

- **Unit turnovers:** clearing left-behind items and refresh debris between tenants (a 15- or 20-yard).
- **Cleanouts:** an abandoned unit or a bulk clear-out.
- **Renovations:** flooring, fixtures, drywall across units.
- **Common-area and grounds work.**

For multiple units, a hauler can coordinate boxes and swaps across turnovers. Confirm placement at each property (and any permit or community rules). Set aside hazardous items and electronics for the county facility. Describe the properties and schedule to the hauler, who can coordinate the boxes.
