# Safe Distribution of Weight in a Dumpster

How weight sits in a dumpster affects whether the truck can lift it safely. This post covers even weight distribution.

## Distributing the Weight

Even weight distribution matters for safety:

- **Spread heavy items across the floor,** not piled at one end, so the box lifts level onto the truck.
- **Load heavy material low and even,** then lighter debris on top.
- **Avoid a lopsided load,** which can shift during lifting or transport and make the box unstable.
- **Keep within the weight allowance,** since an overweight box is both a cost and a lifting-safety issue.
- **Do not concentrate all the weight at the door end or one side.**

A well-distributed load lifts safely and evenly onto the roll-off truck and rides stably; a lopsided or overweight load is harder and less safe to handle. This is both a safety practice and part of staying within the weight allowance. Describe heavy debris to the hauler, who sets the right box so the weight is manageable.
