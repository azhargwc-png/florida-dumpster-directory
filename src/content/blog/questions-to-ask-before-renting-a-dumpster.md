# Questions to Ask Before Renting a Dumpster

A few questions before you book settle the size, the cost drivers and the logistics, so the rental goes smoothly with no surprises. This post is the checklist to run through with any dumpster company.

## The Questions to Ask

**On size and debris:** What size fits my job? Describe the debris and ask them to confirm. Should heavy material go in a separate box?

**On cost:** What does the base price include? What is the weight allowance and the overage rate? How many days are included and what is an extra day? Any special-item surcharges?

**On placement and permits:** Does my placement (driveway, street, swale) need a permit? Who arranges it? What access does the truck need?

**On logistics:** How far ahead should I book? What are the delivery and pickup windows? How do I schedule a swap or extension?

**On restrictions:** What can't go in the box? Where does hazardous waste go?

Running through these turns a booking into a clear agreement.

## Get a Real Number for Your Job

Because the total depends on your specific debris, size, days and location, the only accurate figure is a quote from a local company for your job. Describe the project and they account for every driver and fee, so the number reflects what you will actually pay.
