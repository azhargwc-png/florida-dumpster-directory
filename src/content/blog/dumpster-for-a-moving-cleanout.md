# Dumpster for a Moving Cleanout

A move is a natural time to clear out what you are not taking, and a dumpster handles the volume. This post covers using one for a moving cleanout.

## Clearing Before or After a Move

Moving surfaces everything you own, and a lot of it, worn furniture, broken items, accumulated junk, is not worth taking. A dumpster lets you clear it efficiently:

- **Before the move:** declutter so you pack and move less, which can also lower moving costs.
- **After the move:** clear what the previous occupant left, or what does not fit the new place.
- **At a rental turnover:** clear a unit between tenants.

Moving debris is mostly household goods and furniture, light and bulky, so a 15- or 20-yard box fits most moves, sized by volume. Donate usable items, set aside hazardous materials and electronics for the county facility, and load the rest. Time the box to your packing or move-out so it is there when you are sorting. Describe the move to the hauler for a size.
