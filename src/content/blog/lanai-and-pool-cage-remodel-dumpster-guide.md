# Lanai and Pool-Cage Remodel Dumpster Guide

Lanai and pool-cage work is common in Southwest Florida, and the debris, aluminum framing and screen, is light and bulky. This post covers handling it.

## Lanai and Pool-Cage Debris

Remodeling or replacing a lanai (screened porch) or pool cage, common in Southwest Florida, produces mostly aluminum framing and screen, plus any pavers, concrete or decking involved:

- **Aluminum and screen:** light and very bulky, so they fill volume, not weight. Break the frame into sections so it packs rather than fanning out.
- **Pavers or concrete decking:** heavy, so a separate small box if you remove them.
- **Size:** a 20- or 30-yard box suits the light aluminum and screen, since it is bulky; a small job may fit a 15-yard.

The aluminum has scrap value, so some set it aside for a recycler. See the [demolition dumpster page](/demolition-dumpster-rental/). Describe the lanai or cage, and any concrete/paver work, to the hauler.
