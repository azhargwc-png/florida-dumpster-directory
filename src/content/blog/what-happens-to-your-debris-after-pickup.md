# What Happens to Your Debris After Pickup

After the truck hauls your box away, the debris is weighed, sorted and either recycled or disposed of. This post follows it.

## After the Box Leaves

Once your dumpster is collected:

- **It is weighed** at the disposal or transfer facility, which sets any weight overage.
- **It is sorted** by material where the facility recycles: concrete to aggregate, metal to recycling, clean wood diverted, the rest to disposal.
- **Recyclable material is diverted;** general debris goes to the landfill.
- **In Lee County,** C and D debris is routed to a recycling-capable facility per the mandatory rule.

So your debris does not simply vanish into a landfill, much of it, especially from a clean load, is recycled, while the non-recyclable remainder is disposed of. The weighing at this stage sets the final weight-based cost. Keeping clean loads increases how much is recycled. Describe your debris to the hauler, who routes it appropriately.
