# Can You Put a Toilet in a Dumpster?

Yes, a toilet goes in a dumpster, along with sinks, tubs and other bathroom fixtures. They are porcelain, ceramic, acrylic or cast iron, none of it hazardous. The only prep is to empty any water first. This post covers how to dumpster bathroom fixtures from a remodel.

## Yes, Fixtures Go In

An old toilet, sink, vanity top, bathtub or shower pan is standard bathroom-remodel debris and goes in a general roll-off. Porcelain and ceramic fixtures, acrylic tubs and surrounds, and cast-iron tubs are all accepted. A bathroom gut puts the old fixtures straight in the box with the rest of the demo debris.

## Empty the Water First

Before a toilet goes in, empty it: turn off the supply, flush to drain the tank, and sponge or bail out the remaining water in the tank and bowl, since dumpsters take no liquids. The same goes for any fixture holding water. This is quick but worth doing, both for the no-liquids rule and to avoid sloshing water around as you carry it.

## Watch the Weight on Cast Iron

Most fixtures are light-to-moderate, but a cast-iron bathtub is an exception: it is extremely heavy, often taking two or more people to move, and it adds real weight to a load. Porcelain toilets and sinks are moderate. If you are removing a cast-iron tub, mention it to the hauler, and plan the lift carefully, since these tubs are both heavy and awkward. Some people break a cast-iron tub into pieces with a sledgehammer (wearing eye protection and covering it to contain fragments) to make it manageable.
