# Roofing Dumpster Rental in Southwest Florida

A re-roof is one of the most common dumpster jobs, and in Southwest Florida the shingle-vs-tile distinction drives everything. This pillar post covers roofing dumpster rental.

## Roofing Debris in SWFL

A roof tear-off produces shingles or tile, underlayment, nails and flashing, and sometimes damaged decking. The key factor is the roof type:

- **Shingle:** a 20-yard box, weight allowance set for roofing.
- **Tile (concrete or clay):** a 10-yard box filled partway, since tile is several times heavier.

Roofing is measured in squares (100 sq ft). Give the hauler the roof type and rough square footage. Keep the load clean (just roofing) for weight and recycling. Southwest Florida's mix of tile and shingle roofs, plus frequent storm re-roofs, makes roofing a steady dumpster job. See the [roofing dumpster page](/roofing-dumpster-rental/).
