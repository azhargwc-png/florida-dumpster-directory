# How Construction Debris Is Sorted and Recycled

After a C and D load leaves your dumpster, it is sorted and processed by material. This post follows the debris through recycling.

## From Dumpster to Recycled Material

At a C and D recycling facility, a construction load is sorted and processed:

- **Concrete and masonry** are crushed into aggregate for use in roadbeds and new concrete.
- **Metal** is separated and sent to metal recycling.
- **Wood** (clean, untreated) is chipped for mulch or fuel; treated wood is disposed of.
- **Cardboard and clean recyclables** are diverted.
- **The remainder** goes to disposal.

Clean, single-material loads sort and recycle most efficiently; mixed and contaminated loads yield less and more goes to disposal. This is why keeping heavy material in clean, separate loads matters, and why Lee County's C and D recycling requirement favors clean loads. Describe your debris to the hauler, who routes it to a recycling-capable facility where this sorting happens.
