# Common Dumpster Rental Mistakes to Avoid

Most dumpster rental problems come from a handful of avoidable mistakes. This post lists them and how to avoid each.

## The Common Mistakes

- **Wrong size.** Too small means a second haul; too large wastes money. Fix: describe the job to the hauler, and size up if between two.
- **Underestimating weight.** Heavy debris (concrete, tile, dirt) in a big box causes an overage. Fix: use a small box filled partway for dense material.
- **Overfilling.** A load above the fill line cannot be hauled. Fix: keep it level, use a swap for extra.
- **Banned items.** Hazardous waste, tires, electronics cause a contamination charge. Fix: sort them to the county facility first.
- **Blocked access.** A blocked spot causes a trip fee. Fix: clear and mark the spot.
- **No permit.** A street box without a permit can be fined. Fix: use the driveway or pull the permit.
- **Bad timing.** A box delivered too early sits idle. Fix: time it to your work.

Avoiding these keeps the rental smooth and on budget.
