# What a Directory Does vs a Dumpster Company

A dumpster directory and a dumpster company do different things: the directory helps you find and reach local companies, and the company provides the box and does the work. This post explains the distinction.

## Directory vs Company

**A dumpster company** (a local hauler) owns the roll-off boxes and trucks and does the physical work: delivery, pickup and disposal. They are who you rent from.

**A directory** is an information and connection service. It helps you find dumpster rental companies serving your area, with details like which companies cover your city and their review counts, and connects you with a local operator for your job. The directory does not own boxes or haul debris; it points you to the companies that do.

This site is a directory: it lists companies serving Southwest Florida cities and connects you with a local hauler. Call the number and we help you reach a company that covers your area. The hauler you reach handles the rental, delivery, pickup and disposal.
