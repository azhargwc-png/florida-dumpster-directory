# How Heavy Is a Loaded Dumpster on a Driveway?

A loaded dumpster puts several tons on a few small contact points, which is why driveway protection matters. This post explains the weight and its distribution.

## Weight and Where It Lands

A loaded roll-off is heavy, the box plus its debris can total several tons, and that weight rests on the box's rails or feet, a few small contact points, not spread evenly. That concentration is what threatens a driveway: several tons on a few square inches can crack a paver, mark concrete or dent asphalt. The delivery truck adds more, its wheels putting heavy point loads along its path.

This is why boards matter: they spread the concentrated weight across a broad area, so the load per square inch on the driveway drops well below what cracks or marks the surface. Without boards, the contact points bear the full concentration; with boards, the same total weight is distributed safely.

So the issue is not the total weight alone but how it concentrates, and boards under the contact points and the truck path are the answer. Describe your surface to the hauler, who can advise on protection.
