# What Size Dumpster for a Whole-House Cleanout

Choosing a dumpster size for a whole-house cleanout comes down to the debris type and volume. A whole-house cleanout usually fits a 20-yard box, with a 30-yard for a large or packed home. The debris is mostly light and bulky, so volume governs.

## Sizing A whole-house cleanout

A typical whole-house cleanout fits a 20-yard box, the usual choice. A large home, or one packed with decades of belongings, may need a 30-yard or a swap. Because cleanout debris is mostly light, bulky material (furniture, boxes, general household goods), volume is the limiting factor, which makes a larger box practical. Set hazardous items, electronics and appliances aside for the proper facilities.

## Weight vs Volume

The reason sizing is not just about volume is that a dumpster also has a weight limit. Light, bulky debris fills the volume first; heavy material (concrete, tile, dirt, stone) reaches the weight limit first and goes in a smaller box filled partway. Matching the box to both factors is what avoids an overage charge or a second haul. See the [20-yard dumpster page](/20-yard-dumpster-rental/).

## Confirm With the Hauler

The surest way to size a whole-house cleanout is to describe it to the hauler: the scope, the rooms or square footage, and the kind of debris. The [size calculator](/tools/dumpster-size-calculator/) gives a starting estimate, and the hauler confirms the box and weight allowance from there, so you neither run out of room nor pay for space you do not use.
