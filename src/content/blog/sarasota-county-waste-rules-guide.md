# Sarasota County Waste Rules: Chemical Collection Centers, Leaf It Out and When to Rent a Dumpster

Sarasota County runs a well-developed solid-waste system with two Chemical Collection Centers, a landfill, a distinctive yard-waste rule, and curbside service to more than 170,000 homes. Knowing the rules tells you what curbside covers, where hazardous waste and yard waste go, and when a project needs a dumpster. This guide covers the Chemical Collection Centers, the Leaf It Out rule, the landfill, and the point where a renovation or cleanout moves to a rented roll-off. It applies across Sarasota County, including Sarasota, Venice, North Port, Nokomis, Osprey, Longboat Key and the Sarasota side of Englewood.

## Curbside Collection

Sarasota County provides broad curbside service. (cite index="114-1">Sarasota County provides curbside collection services to more than 170,000 residences, covering garbage, recycling, yard waste, electronics, chemicals and appliances through its programs.</cite> Routine garbage, recycling and yard waste are collected curbside, but renovation and cleanout debris are not, which is where a dumpster comes in.

## The Chemical Collection Centers

Household hazardous waste never goes in a dumpster, and in Sarasota County it goes free to one of two Chemical Collection Centers. (cite index="122-1">Residents of Sarasota County may take accepted items generated from non-business activities to one of the County's Chemical Collection Centers for safe disposal free of charge.</cite> (cite index="114-1">All residents in Sarasota County may use the household hazardous waste disposal services.</cite>

The two centers are in Sarasota and Venice. (cite index="124-1">Hazardous materials and electronics may be taken year-round to the Chemical Collection Center (North) at 8750 Bee Ridge Road, Sarasota, and the Chemical Collection Center (South) at 250 S. Jackson Road, Venice.</cite> One change to note: (cite index="122-1">Pharmaceuticals are no longer accepted at the Sarasota County Chemical Collection Centers, with multiple drop-off locations for prescription drugs available throughout the county.</cite>

Usable items get a second life. (cite index="127-1">Items brought to the Chemical Collection Centers that are still in usable condition make up the inventory found in The Re-Uz-It Shop.</cite>

## The Leaf It Out Yard-Waste Rule

Sarasota County is distinctive for banning plastic bags from yard-waste collection. (cite index="111-1">Sarasota County's Leaf It Out campaign applies to yard waste; effective May 1, 2023, customers bringing yard waste in plastic bags to the landfill must empty the bags, and a minimum garbage fee applies to the plastic bags.</cite> Curbside follows the same rule. (cite index="120-1">Sarasota County does not pick up yard waste left in plastic bags; yard waste must be in reusable containers or paper bags, less than 4 feet in length, under 40 pounds, and 16 inches or less in diameter.</cite> For a large landscaping job beyond those limits, a dumpster is the practical route.

## The Landfill

Sarasota County operates a central landfill. (cite index="113-1">The Central County Solid Waste Disposal Complex (Landfill) is at 4000 Knights Trail Road, Nokomis, with a Citizens' Convenience Center for disposal.</cite> For most homeowners, a dumpster hauler takes project debris to the appropriate facility, so you rarely deal with the landfill directly.

## When a Project Needs a Dumpster in Sarasota County

A roll-off earns its place when a project produces more debris than curbside will take, or debris curbside will not take:

- A kitchen, bath or whole-home renovation.
- A roof replacement, shingle or tile.
- A garage, estate or whole-house cleanout.
- A construction or demolition project.
- A large landscaping tear-out beyond the Leaf It Out curbside limits.

For these, a dumpster company serving Sarasota County handles delivery and hauling. Keep hazardous waste, electronics and appliances out and take them to a Chemical Collection Center.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Where do I take household hazardous waste in Sarasota County?**
To one of the two Chemical Collection Centers, free for residents: the North center at 8750 Bee Ridge Road, Sarasota, or the South center at 250 S. Jackson Road, Venice. Both accept hazardous materials and electronics year-round. They take paint, chemicals, solvents, pool chemicals, batteries and similar items. Pharmaceuticals are no longer accepted there, so use one of the county's prescription-drug drop-off locations instead. None of these items go in a dumpster, so separate them before loading a roll-off.

**What is the Leaf It Out rule in Sarasota County?**
Leaf It Out is Sarasota County's rule banning plastic bags from yard-waste disposal. Effective May 1, 2023, yard waste set out curbside or brought to the landfill in plastic bags is not collected as yard waste; it must be in reusable containers or paper bags, less than 4 feet long, under 40 pounds, and 16 inches or less in diameter. For a large landscaping tear-out beyond those limits, a rented dumpster is the practical route rather than many small bagged loads.

**Where is the Sarasota County landfill?**
The Central County Solid Waste Disposal Complex, the county landfill, is at 4000 Knights Trail Road, Nokomis, and includes a Citizens' Convenience Center for resident disposal. For most homeowners, a dumpster hauler takes project debris to the appropriate facility, so you rarely deal with the landfill directly. If you are self-hauling, remember the Leaf It Out rule for yard waste and that hazardous materials and electronics go to a Chemical Collection Center, not the landfill.

**What size dumpster do I need for a Sarasota County project?**
It depends on the job and the debris weight. A cleanout or shingle re-roof usually fits a 20-yard box, a bathroom remodel a 15-yard, and a whole-home renovation a 20- or 30-yard. Heavy debris like tile, concrete or masonry goes in a smaller 10-yard box filled partway, since weight hits the limit before the box looks full. Confirm the size by weight with a dumpster company serving Sarasota County, and keep hazardous waste, electronics and appliances out, routing them to a Chemical Collection Center.
