# What Is a Roll-Off Truck and How It Delivers

A roll-off truck is the specialized truck that delivers and collects roll-off dumpsters by tilting its bed and rolling the box on and off. Understanding how it works tells you what access it needs. This post explains it.

## How the Truck Works

A roll-off truck has a hydraulic tilting bed and a cable or hook system. To deliver, it backs up to the spot, tilts the bed, and lets the box roll off on its rear wheels onto the ground. To collect, it tilts the bed, winches the box back up the rails onto the bed, and levels off to drive away. Because of this, the truck needs:

- **A clear, straight approach** to back in.
- **Overhead clearance,** since the bed tilts high (no low branches or wires).
- **A firm, reasonably level surface** that can take the truck and the loaded box.
- **Room behind the spot** for the truck to position.

If the truck cannot get this access, it cannot place or collect the box, which is why confirming access when you book, and clearing the spot, matters.
