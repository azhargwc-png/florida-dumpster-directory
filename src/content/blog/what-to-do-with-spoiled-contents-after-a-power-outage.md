# What to Do With Spoiled Contents After a Power Outage

A long post-storm power outage spoils refrigerated and frozen food and can ruin other contents. Disposing of it is part of storm cleanup, and a dumpster helps for larger volumes. This post covers it.

## Handling Spoiled Contents

A prolonged outage spoils food and can ruin stored contents. For disposal:

- **Bag spoiled food securely** in sealed trash bags to contain odor and leakage; it goes in regular trash or, for large volumes, a dumpster. Do not put loose food waste or liquids directly in a roll-off.
- **Ruined contents** (soaked or damaged furniture, boxes, bedding) go in a dumpster for a larger cleanout, especially combined with other storm debris.
- **Spoiled contents in a dead refrigerator:** empty and bag the food first; the appliance itself needs refrigerant recovery if it is being discarded.
- **Check county guidance,** since some post-storm programs address spoiled-food disposal specifically.

For a few bags, regular collection handles it; for a large cleanout of ruined contents, a dumpster is the practical tool.
