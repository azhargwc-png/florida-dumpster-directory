# What Size Dumpster for Yard Waste

Choosing a dumpster size for yard waste comes down to the debris type and volume. Yard waste sizing depends on volume: a 15-yard for a modest cleanup, a 20-yard for a larger one. Note that Sarasota County's Leaf It Out rule affects bagging for curbside, but a dumpster handles large jobs.

## Sizing Yard waste

A modest landscaping cleanup fits a 15-yard box, and a larger tear-out a 20-yard. Yard waste (branches, brush, palm fronds, sod) is bulky, so volume governs, though a lot of wet sod or soil adds weight. For routine yard waste, county curbside collection may suffice (note Sarasota County's Leaf It Out plastic-bag rule), but a large tree removal or landscaping overhaul is a dumpster job.

## Weight vs Volume

The reason sizing is not just about volume is that a dumpster also has a weight limit. Light, bulky debris fills the volume first; heavy material (concrete, tile, dirt, stone) reaches the weight limit first and goes in a smaller box filled partway. Matching the box to both factors is what avoids an overage charge or a second haul. See the [yard waste dumpster page](/yard-waste-dumpster-rental/).

## Confirm With the Hauler

The surest way to size yard waste is to describe it to the hauler: the scope, the rooms or square footage, and the kind of debris. The [size calculator](/tools/dumpster-size-calculator/) gives a starting estimate, and the hauler confirms the box and weight allowance from there, so you neither run out of room nor pay for space you do not use.
