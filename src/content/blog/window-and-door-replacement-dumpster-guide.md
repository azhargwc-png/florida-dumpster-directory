# Window and Door Replacement Dumpster Guide

A window or door replacement produces old frames, glass and doors, a common project in storm-prone Southwest Florida. This post covers handling the debris.

## Window and Door Debris

Replacing windows and doors, common in Southwest Florida where impact-rated upgrades are frequent, produces old frames (aluminum or vinyl), glass, and old doors:

- **Glass:** contain broken glass safely; impact (laminated) glass cracks rather than shattering, so it is a bit safer. See the glass post.
- **Frames:** aluminum and vinyl are light; some have scrap value.
- **Doors:** wood, metal or fiberglass, bulky but manageable.
- **Size:** a whole-house window-and-door replacement often fits a 15- or 20-yard box, sized by volume, especially combined with other debris; a few windows may fit a smaller box or ride in a general renovation load.

Describe the number of windows and doors to the hauler for a size.
