# Dumpster Placement and Underground Utilities

A heavy dumpster can damage what is under the placement spot, septic components, irrigation lines, shallow utilities. This post covers placing safely around underground features.

## What Lies Beneath the Spot

A loaded dumpster concentrates several tons on its contact points, so what is under the spot matters:

- **Septic systems.** Avoid placing a box over a septic tank, drain field or lines, common on rural and older Southwest Florida lots, since the weight can crack components or compact the drain field.
- **Irrigation and sprinkler lines.** Shallow lines under a lawn or near a driveway edge can be crushed.
- **Shallow utilities and well lines.** On some properties, water, well or utility lines run close to the surface.
- **Driveway edges and culverts.** The swale culvert and driveway apron can be damaged by the truck.

The safest placement is a firm driveway away from these features. If you are unsure what is under a spot, especially a septic field, keep the box on the paved driveway. Knowing your property's layout, or checking with a locate service for a dig-adjacent concern, helps you place the box where it will not cause damage.
