# Dumpster for Roof Damage After a Storm

Storm roof damage means a tear-off and replacement, which is a roofing dumpster job sized to the roof type. Tile is far heavier than shingle. This post covers it.

## Sizing a Storm Roof Job

A storm-damaged roof usually needs a tear-off and re-roof, generating roofing debris, shingles or tile, underlayment, and damaged decking. Match the box to the roof:

- **Shingle roof:** a 20-yard box, weight allowance set for roofing.
- **Tile roof:** a 10-yard box filled partway, since concrete and clay tile are several times heavier.

Tell the hauler the roof type and rough square footage. Roofing debris from storm repair is rebuild/construction debris, so it goes in a private dumpster, distinct from the county's curbside storm collection. Keep the load clean (just roofing) for weight and recycling. If the whole house is being rebuilt, the roof debris may be one of several loads or a swap.
