# How to Separate Hurricane Debris in Florida

County storm-debris collection only works if residents separate debris into the right piles. Getting the separation right keeps your debris eligible. This post covers the standard piles and what a private dumpster handles instead.

## The Separation Rules

After a declared storm, counties collect eligible debris from the right-of-way, but only if it is separated into distinct piles. The standard categories are:

- **Vegetative (yard) debris:** branches, limbs, leaves, palm fronds.
- **Construction and demolition debris:** drywall, lumber, roofing, flooring, fencing.
- **Appliances and white goods:** set out separately, not mixed in.
- **Electronics:** separate.
- **Household hazardous waste:** never in a pile, goes to the county facility.

Keep piles clear of electrical lines, water meters, mailboxes and fire hydrants, and do not bag debris in black bags (counties require visible debris). A private dumpster handles private-property, rebuild and commercial debris that you are not setting at the right-of-way, so hazardous waste and appliances stay out of it too.
