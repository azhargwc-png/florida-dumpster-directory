# What Size Dumpster for a Kitchen Remodel

Choosing a dumpster size for a kitchen remodel comes down to the debris type and volume. A kitchen remodel usually fits a 15- or 20-yard box. Cabinets drive the volume; stone countertops drive the weight.

## Sizing A kitchen remodel

A typical kitchen remodel fits a 15- or 20-yard box. Cabinets are bulky but light and drive the volume, so break them down to pack efficiently. Stone countertops (granite or quartz) are heavy and drive the weight, so mention them to the hauler. A full kitchen gut with flooring and tile may need a 20-yard.

## Weight vs Volume

The reason sizing is not just about volume is that a dumpster also has a weight limit. Light, bulky debris fills the volume first; heavy material (concrete, tile, dirt, stone) reaches the weight limit first and goes in a smaller box filled partway. Matching the box to both factors is what avoids an overage charge or a second haul. See the [20-yard dumpster page](/20-yard-dumpster-rental/).

## Confirm With the Hauler

The surest way to size a kitchen remodel is to describe it to the hauler: the scope, the rooms or square footage, and the kind of debris. The [size calculator](/tools/dumpster-size-calculator/) gives a starting estimate, and the hauler confirms the box and weight allowance from there, so you neither run out of room nor pay for space you do not use.
