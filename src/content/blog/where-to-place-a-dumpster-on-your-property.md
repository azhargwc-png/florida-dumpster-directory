# Where to Place a Dumpster on Your Property

Choosing where the dumpster goes balances truck access, the surface, permit avoidance and loading convenience. This post covers how to pick the spot.

## Choosing the Spot

The best placement usually meets several criteria:

- **On your driveway (private property),** to avoid a right-of-way permit.
- **Reachable by the truck,** with a clear, straight approach and overhead clearance (no low branches or wires).
- **On a firm, level surface** that can take the loaded box.
- **Convenient for loading,** close to your work, with the door-end facing where you will carry debris.
- **Protected if it is pavers** or a finished surface, with boards under the contact points.
- **Not blocking** what you need (a second vehicle, a gate, access to the house).

If the driveway cannot meet these, the alternatives are the street or swale (with a permit) or, in a community, a spot the HOA allows. Mark the chosen spot for the driver and confirm it works when you book. A local hauler can advise on placement for a tricky lot.
