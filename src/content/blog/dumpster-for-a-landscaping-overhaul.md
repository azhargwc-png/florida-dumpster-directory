# Dumpster for a Landscaping Overhaul

A full landscaping overhaul removes plants, sod, hardscape and more, mixing light and heavy debris. This post covers planning it.

## Planning an Overhaul

A landscaping overhaul, redoing a yard, produces a mix across the project: plants, shrubs and trees (light, bulky), sod and soil (heavy), and often hardscape, pavers, stone, edging (heavy). To plan it:

- **Separate light from heavy.** Plant material in a general box (15- or 20-yard); sod, soil, pavers and stone in a separate small box filled partway.
- **Consider the volume.** A whole-yard overhaul can produce a lot, so a swap or multiple hauls may fit.
- **Time to the work.** Demolition of the old landscape produces a burst; install produces packaging and offcuts.

Describe the overhaul, and the split of plant vs heavy material, to the hauler, who plans the boxes and any swaps. See the [yard waste dumpster page](/yard-waste-dumpster-rental/).
