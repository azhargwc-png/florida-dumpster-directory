# Why Dumpster Prices Vary by City

The same size dumpster can be quoted differently in two Southwest Florida cities. The differences come from distance, local disposal costs, permits and demand, not from any single list price. This post explains why location affects the quote.

## Why Location Affects the Price

Several location factors move the price between cities:

- **Distance from the hauler's yard.** Delivery and haul distance is a direct cost, so a remote, rural or island location (LaBelle, Sanibel, Marco Island) can cost more than one close to the yard.
- **Local disposal costs.** Tipping fees and the distance to the nearest landfill or transfer station vary by county, which flows into the price.
- **Permits.** Some cities require a right-of-way permit for street placement, an added cost that varies by city.
- **Local demand and competition.** Areas with more haulers and competition may price differently from areas with few.

So a quote reflects your specific location as much as the box, which is why the only accurate number is a local quote for your address.

## Get a Real Number for Your Job

Because the total depends on your specific debris, size, days and location, the only accurate figure is a quote from a local company for your job. Describe the project and they account for every driver and fee, so the number reflects what you will actually pay.
