# Best Time to Rent a Dumpster After a Hurricane

After a hurricane, a private dumpster is the right tool for rebuild and private-property debris, separate from the county's curbside storm collection. The best time to rent depends on your cleanup phase and the demand surge. This post covers timing.

## Timing Your Rental

A private dumpster never competes with the county's curbside storm-debris program, which collects eligible debris set at the right-of-way after a declared event. Use the county program for that; use a private dumpster for private-property debris, rebuild and repair work, commercial cleanups, and anything you cannot wait on.

On timing:

- **Immediately after the storm,** demand surges and boxes are scarce, so book as early as you can if you need one.
- **For rebuild work,** rent when the repair phase starts and you are generating construction debris, which is distinct from the initial storm debris.
- **Book ahead,** since lead times lengthen across the region after a storm.

Keep hazardous waste and appliances out of the box, and route eligible yard and storm debris to the county program.
