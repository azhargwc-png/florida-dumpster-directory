# 15-Yard vs 20-Yard Dumpster

The 15-yard and 20-yard are the two most common renovation and cleanout sizes. The 20-yard is the versatile default; the 15-yard is a step down for a more contained job. The difference is purely volume, since both handle the same light-to-moderate debris.

## The 15-Yard

Best for a single-room or contained job. Typical jobs: a bathroom or small kitchen remodel, a garage cleanout, a one- or two-room flooring tear-out.

## The 20-Yard

Best for a whole-house or multi-room job. Typical jobs: a whole-house cleanout, a shingle re-roof, a multi-room renovation, a home-wide flooring tear-out.

## Which to Choose

Choose the 15-yard for a contained, single-area project, and the 20-yard when the job spans the whole house or several rooms. When in doubt between the two, most people size up to the 20-yard, since running out of room mid-project is more costly than a little unused space, and the 20-yard is only modestly larger.

Describe your project to the hauler, the type of job, the rooms or square footage, and the kind of debris, and they will confirm the right size. The [size calculator](/tools/dumpster-size-calculator/) is a good starting point.
