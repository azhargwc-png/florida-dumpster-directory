# Dumpster for a Rental Property Turnover

A rental turnover means clearing what a departing tenant left and any repair debris before the next tenant. A dumpster handles both. This post covers it.

## Clearing Between Tenants

A turnover combines two debris sources: items the departing tenant left (furniture, belongings, trash) and any repair or refresh debris (old flooring, paint, fixtures, drywall). A dumpster handles both:

- **Size by volume.** A 15- or 20-yard box fits most turnovers; a larger unit or a bigger refresh may need a 20-yard or more.
- **Clear placement with the property.** Confirm where the box can sit at a rental or in a community, and any HOA or park rules.
- **Set aside hazardous items and electronics** for the county facility.
- **Time it to the turnover window** between tenants, when the unit is empty and before the next move-in.

For a property manager handling multiple units, a hauler can coordinate boxes across turnovers. Describe the turnover to the hauler for a size.
