# North Fort Myers Dumpster Permit and Placement Rules

Renting a dumpster in North Fort Myers, FL means knowing who governs placement, where a box can sit, and where your debris goes. This guide covers the permit question, placement, and the local Lee County facilities.

## Who Governs Placement in North Fort Myers

North Fort Myers is an unincorporated community in Lee County, so Lee County governs placement. A box on a public right-of-way follows county rules; a box on your own driveway generally needs no permit.

## Placement and Local Factors

North Fort Myers is a mix of established single-family, waterfront and mobile-home communities, many with driveway or lot room for private placement. For a mobile-home community, the park owner controls placement.

**On your own driveway:** a box entirely on your private driveway usually needs no permit, which is the simplest option. Protect a paver or finished driveway with boards under the roll-off's contact points.

**On the street or right-of-way:** a box there follows the rules of whoever owns the road, so confirm the current requirement before placing one.

## Where Your Debris Goes

North Fort Myers uses the Lee County Topaz Court facility at 6441 Topaz Court, Fort Myers for household hazardous waste and electronics, free for residents. The county's mandatory C and D recycling applies. Keep hazardous waste, electronics and appliances out of the dumpster.

Yes, but placement needs coordination. For a cleanout or renovation in one of North Fort Myers' many mobile-home or manufactured-home communities, the park or community owner controls where a box can sit and for how long, so clear the placement with management before booking. Choose a size that fits the available space, and confirm truck access. The Lee County waste rules, including hazardous-waste drop-off at Topaz Court, still apply.

**What size dumpster do I need in North Fort Myers?**
It depends on the job and the debris weight. A cleanout or shingle re-roof usually fits a 20-yard box, a bathroom remodel a 15-yard, and a whole-home renovation a 20- or 30-yard. Heavy debris like concrete or tile goes in a smaller 10-yard box filled partway, since weight hits the limit before the box looks full. Confirm the size by weight with a dumpster company serving North Fort Myers, and keep hazardous waste, electronics and appliances out of the box.
