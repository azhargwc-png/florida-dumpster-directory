# Dumpster Rules Across Lee County Cities

Lee County shares one solid-waste system, but dumpster placement and permit rules can differ between its incorporated cities and the unincorporated areas. Knowing who governs your address tells you whether a street-placed box needs a city or county permit. This post walks through how the rules vary across Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Bonita Springs, Estero, the islands and unincorporated Lee County, and what stays the same everywhere.

## What Is the Same Countywide

Some things do not change by city. Hazardous waste, electronics and appliances all route to the same place. (cite index="64-1">Cape Coral residents dispose of household hazardous waste through Lee County's permanent facility at 6441 Topaz Court, Fort Myers.</cite> (cite index="66-1">Estero's garbage, recycling and yard waste is handled by Lee County Solid Waste.</cite> The county's mandatory C and D recycling requirement also applies across Lee County. And a box entirely on your own private driveway generally needs no permit anywhere, since it is on private property.

What changes is the rule for a box on a street or public right-of-way, which depends on who owns the road.

## Incorporated Cities

**Fort Myers and Cape Coral** are incorporated, so a box on a city street or right-of-way follows city rules and usually needs a city permit. On your own driveway, no permit is typically required. Cape Coral is heavily platted with canal lots, so confirm truck access and placement on a tight lot. Both cities still use the county's Topaz Court facility for hazardous waste.

**Bonita Springs** is an incorporated city in south Lee County. A street or right-of-way box follows city rules; a driveway box usually does not need a permit. Gated and deed-restricted communities are common here and may add their own placement and duration rules.

## Unincorporated Communities

**Estero** is an incorporated village but relies on Lee County Solid Waste for collection, so a street-placed box follows the applicable right-of-way rules; a driveway box generally does not need a permit.

**Lehigh Acres and North Fort Myers** are unincorporated, so a box on a public right-of-way follows Lee County rules. Many lots here have room for private placement, which is the simplest route.

## The Islands

**Sanibel and Fort Myers Beach** are island communities with their own characteristics. Sanibel is an incorporated city with strict environmental and placement standards, and access comes over a causeway or bridge, so confirm delivery logistics and any island-specific rules. Fort Myers Beach, also incorporated and rebuilding from recent storms, has tight barrier-island lots and its own placement rules. On both, clear placement carefully and plan for island access.

## The Practical Rule

Across every Lee County city, the decision comes down to one question: is the box on your own driveway, or on a public street or right-of-way? Driveway placement usually needs no permit anywhere. Street or right-of-way placement needs a permit from whoever owns the road, the city in an incorporated area or Lee County in an unincorporated one. A dumpster company that serves your specific Lee County city will know which permit applies and can advise on placement. Hazardous waste, electronics and appliances always go to Topaz Court, countywide.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do dumpster permit rules differ between Lee County cities?**
The rule for a box on your own driveway is the same everywhere: it generally needs no permit, since it is on private property. The difference is for a box on a public street or right-of-way, where the permit comes from whoever owns the road, an incorporated city like Fort Myers, Cape Coral or Bonita Springs, or Lee County in an unincorporated area like Lehigh Acres or North Fort Myers. Confirm who governs your address, and a local hauler will know which permit applies.

**Does hazardous waste disposal differ by Lee County city?**
No. Hazardous waste, electronics and appliances route to the same place countywide: the Lee County Topaz Court facility at 6441 Topaz Court, Fort Myers. This covers incorporated cities that contract with Lee County Solid Waste, such as Cape Coral and Estero, as well as the unincorporated areas. So wherever you are in Lee County, keep these items out of a dumpster and take them to Topaz Court, free for residents.

**Are the island cities different for dumpster rental?**
Somewhat. Sanibel is an incorporated city with strict environmental and placement standards, and access comes over a causeway, so confirm delivery logistics and island-specific rules. Fort Myers Beach, also incorporated and rebuilding from recent storms, has tight barrier-island lots and its own placement rules. On both islands, plan placement and access carefully and use a hauler experienced with island delivery. The countywide rules for hazardous waste and C and D recycling still apply.

**Who do I ask about dumpster rules in my Lee County city?**
Start with where the box will sit. For a driveway box, you usually need no permit. For a street or right-of-way box, the permit comes from your city (in Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Bonita Springs, Sanibel or Fort Myers Beach) or from Lee County (in unincorporated areas like Lehigh Acres and North Fort Myers). A dumpster rental company that serves your city will know the current requirement and can handle placement advice, so calling a local hauler is the quickest way to confirm.
