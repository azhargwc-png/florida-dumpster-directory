# Fort Myers Beach Dumpster Permit and Placement Rules

Renting a dumpster in Fort Myers Beach, FL means knowing who governs placement, where a box can sit, and where your debris goes. This guide covers the permit question, placement, and the local Lee County facilities.

## Who Governs Placement in Fort Myers Beach

Fort Myers Beach is an incorporated barrier-island town in Lee County, so the Town of Fort Myers Beach governs placement. A box on a town street or right-of-way follows town rules; a box on your own driveway generally needs none.

## Placement and Local Factors

Fort Myers Beach is a barrier island with tight lots and bridge access, and it is heavily rebuilding from recent storms, so placement, access and delivery scheduling all need planning. Confirm island-specific requirements with the town.

**On your own driveway:** a box entirely on your private driveway usually needs no permit, which is the simplest option. Protect a paver or finished driveway with boards under the roll-off's contact points.

**On the street or right-of-way:** a box there follows the rules of whoever owns the road, so confirm the current requirement before placing one.

## Where Your Debris Goes

Fort Myers Beach uses the Lee County facilities on the mainland: household hazardous waste and electronics go free to the Topaz Court facility at 6441 Topaz Court, Fort Myers. The county's mandatory C and D recycling applies. Keep hazardous waste, electronics and appliances out of the dumpster, and plan the bridge trip.
