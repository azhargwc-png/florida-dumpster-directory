# Do You Need to Be Home for Dumpster Delivery?

You often do not need to be home for dumpster delivery or pickup, as long as the spot is clear, accessible and marked. This post covers when you do need to be present and how to arrange an unattended drop.

## When You Need to Be Present (and When You Don't)

Many deliveries and pickups happen with no one home. You generally do **not** need to be present if:

- The placement spot is clear, accessible and clearly marked.
- No gate or locked access needs to be opened.
- You have told the company the exact spot and orientation you want.

You generally **do** need to be available (or arrange access) if:

- A gate, code or locked area must be opened for the truck.
- Placement needs your direction on the day.
- A condo or community requires someone to meet the driver.

The simplest unattended drop is a clear, marked driveway spot. Mark it (cones, a note, tape) and confirm with the company that no one needs to be present. The same applies to pickup: a clear, accessible, ready box can be collected whether or not you are there.
