# Dumpster for a Pool Removal

A pool removal produces a lot of heavy concrete or gunite plus lighter shell and cage material, so it is typically a two-box job. This post covers sizing it. (See also the pool-debris item post.)

## Sizing a Pool Removal

Removing an in-ground pool generates several materials: the concrete, gunite or shotcrete shell and surrounding deck (heavy), a fiberglass or vinyl liner (light, bulky), the aluminum screen enclosure if present (light), and plumbing and equipment. Because of the weight split:

- **Heavy material** (gunite shell, concrete deck, pavers) goes in a small 10-yard box filled partway, treated as a clean-concrete load where possible.
- **Light material** (fiberglass shell, screen aluminum, general debris) goes in a larger box sized for volume.
- **Pool chemicals** never go in the box; they go to the county hazardous-waste program.

So a full pool removal is typically a two-box job (a small heavy box plus a larger light one) or careful staging with swaps. Describe the pool, its construction and deck, to the hauler, who will size the boxes. See the [concrete dumpster page](/concrete-dumpster-rental/).
