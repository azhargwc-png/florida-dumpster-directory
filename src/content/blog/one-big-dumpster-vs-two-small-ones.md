# One Big Dumpster vs Two Small Ones

For a big or mixed job, you can rent one large dumpster or two smaller ones. The right choice depends on weight, access, and whether you need to separate materials. This post weighs the trade-offs.

## When Two Smaller Boxes Win

Two smaller boxes (or a swap) often make sense when:

- **The job mixes heavy and light debris.** A small 10-yard for concrete or tile (filled partway for weight) plus a larger box for light debris is often better than forcing everything into one, which risks an overweight load.
- **Access is tight.** A smaller box fits a narrow driveway or tight lot where a 30- or 40-yard cannot.
- **You want a clean recyclable load.** Keeping concrete or yard waste separate can help recycling and weight.

One large box wins when the debris is uniformly light and bulky and access allows it, since one delivery and haul is simpler than two. Describe the job to the hauler, who can advise whether one box or two fits best, including a swap (a full box hauled and an empty one dropped).

## Get a Real Number for Your Job

Because the total depends on your specific debris, size, days and location, the only accurate figure is a quote from a local company for your job. Describe the project and they account for every driver and fee, so the number reflects what you will actually pay.
