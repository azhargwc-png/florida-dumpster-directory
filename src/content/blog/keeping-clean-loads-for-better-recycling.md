# Keeping Clean Loads for Better Recycling

A clean, single-material load recycles far better than a mixed one. This post covers keeping clean loads.

## Keeping Loads Clean

A clean load, one material with no contamination, recycles efficiently; a mixed load yields less. To keep loads clean:

- **Separate heavy clean material.** Concrete in its own box (becomes aggregate), not mixed with wood and trash.
- **Keep metal separable.** Clean metal is readily recycled.
- **Keep clean wood apart from treated wood** and trash.
- **Avoid contaminating recyclables** with general trash, food or hazardous residue.
- **Use separate boxes or swaps** for distinct material streams on a big job.

The payoff: more material recycled, potentially lower disposal cost (recycled material can be cheaper to process than landfilled trash), and compliance with Lee County's C and D recycling requirement. The cleaner the load, the more is recovered. Describe your debris to the hauler, who can plan clean loads.
