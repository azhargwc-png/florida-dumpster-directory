# Donate, Sell or Dump: Sorting a Cleanout

The key to an efficient, responsible cleanout is sorting: not everything belongs in the dumpster. This post covers how to sort into donate, sell, dump and hazardous piles.

## The Four Piles (Plus the County Facility)

Sorting a cleanout into clear categories keeps usable items out of the landfill and the dumpster filled with actual trash:

- **Keep / give to family:** anything of value or meaning.
- **Sell:** valuable furniture, collectibles, working appliances, via resale or consignment.
- **Donate:** usable furniture, housewares, clothing and goods, a charity pickup keeps them out of the landfill and can collect larger items.
- **Dump (the dumpster):** worn, broken, outdated and unwanted items not worth keeping, selling or donating.
- **County facility:** hazardous materials (paint, chemicals, oil, propane, batteries), electronics, tires and appliances, which go in none of the above piles and never in the dumpster.

Sorting first means the box fills efficiently with trash, you recover value and do some good with the usable items, and the restricted items are handled correctly. Describe the cleanout to the hauler for a right-sized box for the trash pile.
