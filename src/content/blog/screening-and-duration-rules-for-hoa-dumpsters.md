# Screening and Duration Rules for HOA Dumpsters

Two HOA rules most often affect dumpsters: how long the box can stay and whether it must be screened. This post covers complying with both.

## Screening and Duration

The two HOA rules that most often shape a dumpster rental:

- **Duration:** many HOAs limit how many days a box can stay, so the box must be gone by the deadline. Match the rental period to the HOA limit, and call for prompt pickup when done.
- **Screening:** some HOAs require the box be screened from view (behind a fence, gate, or visual barrier) while it is on site, especially in upscale communities.

To comply: check both rules before booking, choose a rental period within the duration limit, plan any screening, and keep the box on your driveway (which also meets most placement rules). If the project might run past the duration limit, talk to the HOA about an extension. Describe the community's rules to the hauler so delivery and pickup fit.
