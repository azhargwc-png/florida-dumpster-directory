# Why You Cannot Overfill a Dumpster

A dumpster has a fill line, and debris must stay level with it, not heaped above. This post explains why, and what to do if you have more debris than fits.

## The Fill Line Rule

Debris must stay level with the box's fill line (at the top of the walls), not mounded above it, for two reasons:

- **Safety and law.** A truck cannot legally or safely transport a load heaped over the sides; debris can fall onto the road. An overfilled box may simply not be hauled until the excess is removed.
- **The tarp.** The driver tarps the load for transport, which requires it to sit below the rim.

So overfilling is not allowed, and an overfilled box can mean a refused pickup, a request to remove the excess, or an overfill fee. If you have more debris than fits level with the fill line:

- **Arrange a swap** (a full box hauled, an empty one dropped).
- **Add a second box.**
- **Size up next time,** or describe the job more fully so the right size is chosen.

Never heap a box above the fill line; plan for the volume instead.
