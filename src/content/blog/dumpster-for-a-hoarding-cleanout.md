# Dumpster for a Hoarding Cleanout

A hoarding cleanout involves a very high volume of mixed material, often needing multiple hauls or swaps. This post covers the dumpster side of the job, with care and respect.

## Handling a High-Volume Cleanout

A hoarding cleanout can fill several dumpster loads, since the volume is far beyond a normal cleanout. The dumpster side:

- **Expect high volume.** Plan a 20- or 30-yard box with swaps, or multiple hauls, rather than one box.
- **Sort as you go.** Separate items to keep or return to family, donate usable goods, and set aside hazardous materials, electronics, and anything restricted for the county facility.
- **Watch for hidden hazards.** Deep clutter can hide spoiled food, chemicals, sharps or pests, so sort carefully.
- **Allow time.** A hoarding cleanout takes longer, so a rental period with swaps, or a staged plan, fits better than a single short rental.

These cleanouts are often emotionally difficult and may involve a specialist cleaning service; the dumpster handles the disposal volume. Describe the scope to the hauler, who can plan the boxes and swaps.
