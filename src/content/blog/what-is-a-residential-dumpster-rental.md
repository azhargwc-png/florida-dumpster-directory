# What Is a Residential Dumpster Rental?

A residential dumpster rental is a roll-off box rented by a homeowner for a home project, as opposed to an ongoing commercial container. This post explains what it is and its common uses.

## Residential vs Commercial

A residential dumpster rental is a temporary roll-off delivered to a home for a specific project, then hauled away when done. It differs from commercial service in a few ways:

- **Temporary, not ongoing.** It is for one project, not a permanent bin on a route.
- **Project-sized.** Usually a 10 to 20 yard box for a home job, rather than a large commercial container.
- **Driveway placement.** It typically sits on the homeowner's driveway.

Common residential uses include a renovation, a roof replacement, a garage or whole-house cleanout, a flooring tear-out, a deck or fence removal, a landscaping project, and storm-rebuild debris. The box is sized to the home project and the debris, and the rental covers delivery, a set period, pickup and disposal.
