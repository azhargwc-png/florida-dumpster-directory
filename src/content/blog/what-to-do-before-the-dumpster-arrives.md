# What to Do Before the Dumpster Arrives

A little preparation before the box arrives makes everything that follows easier. This post is the focused pre-arrival checklist.

## Before Arrival

Before the dumpster arrives:

- **Confirm the size** fits the job (describe it to the hauler if unsure).
- **Choose and clear the spot** (driveway is simplest), and the truck's approach.
- **Check overhead** for branches and wires.
- **Lay boards** on a paver or finished driveway.
- **Mark the placement** and orientation.
- **Confirm any permit** for a street or swale spot.
- **Sort banned items** aside for the county facility, so loading is clean.
- **Plan your loading** so the box fills steadily.

With this done, delivery is quick, the box lands where you need it, and loading starts right away. The preparation is mostly about the placement spot and sorting out what cannot go in, the two things that most often cause problems if left to delivery day.
