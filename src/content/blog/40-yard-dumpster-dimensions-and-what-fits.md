# 40-Yard Dumpster: Dimensions and What Fits

The 40-yard dumpster holds about 40 cubic yards, roughly twelve to sixteen pickup-truck loads. This post covers its dimensions, what fits, and the projects it suits.

## Dimensions and Capacity

A 40-yard roll-off holds about 40 cubic yards, roughly twelve to sixteen pickup-truck loads. Exact dimensions vary by hauler, but a typical 40-yard box is around 22 feet long, 8 feet wide and 8 feet high. See the [40-yard dumpster page](/40-yard-dumpster-rental/).

## What the 40-Yard Is Best For

The 40-yard is the largest common roll-off, used mostly for commercial and large construction jobs. Its very tall walls make it suited to high volumes of light, bulky debris (such as packaging, cardboard, or lightweight demolition material) rather than heavy debris, which would overload it long before it filled.

Typical jobs for a 40-yard include a large commercial cleanout, major new construction, a whole-building demolition, or a very large renovation producing high volumes of light, bulky debris.

## Weight Still Matters

Whatever the size, a dumpster has a weight limit as well as a volume limit. The 40-yard is sized for volume, so it suits light-to-moderate debris; heavy material like concrete, tile and dirt still goes in a small 10-yard box filled partway, because it would overload a larger box long before filling it. Describe your debris to the hauler so the size and weight allowance match the job.

## How to Be Sure It Is the Right Size

The safest way to size a box is to describe the project to the hauler: the type of job, the rooms or square footage involved, and the kind of debris. A box too small means a second haul or an overfill problem; a box too large means paying for space you do not use. The [size calculator](/tools/dumpster-size-calculator/) is a good starting point, and the hauler confirms from there.
