# What Size Dumpster for a Roof Replacement

Choosing a dumpster size for a roof replacement comes down to the debris type and volume. A roof replacement needs a box sized to the roof type and square footage. A shingle tear-off usually fits a 20-yard; a tile roof needs a 10-yard filled partway because tile is far heavier.

## Sizing A roof replacement

A single-family asphalt shingle re-roof usually fits a 20-yard box with the weight allowance set for roofing. A tile roof (concrete or clay) is several times heavier per square, so a tile tear-off goes in a 10-yard box filled partway. Give the hauler the rough square footage (roofing is measured in squares of 100 square feet) and the roof type.

## Weight vs Volume

The reason sizing is not just about volume is that a dumpster also has a weight limit. Light, bulky debris fills the volume first; heavy material (concrete, tile, dirt, stone) reaches the weight limit first and goes in a smaller box filled partway. Matching the box to both factors is what avoids an overage charge or a second haul. See the [roofing dumpster page](/roofing-dumpster-rental/).

## Confirm With the Hauler

The surest way to size a roof replacement is to describe it to the hauler: the scope, the rooms or square footage, and the kind of debris. The [size calculator](/tools/dumpster-size-calculator/) gives a starting estimate, and the hauler confirms the box and weight allowance from there, so you neither run out of room nor pay for space you do not use.
