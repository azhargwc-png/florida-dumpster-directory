# How to Make the Most of Your Rental Period

You pay for the rental period whether or not the box is working, so timing it well avoids wasted days. This post covers making the most of the period.

## Using the Period Well

To get full value from the rental days:

- **Time delivery to your work.** Have the debris ready or the demolition scheduled before the box arrives, so it fills steadily rather than sitting idle.
- **Do not deliver too early.** A box delivered before you are ready burns rental days doing nothing.
- **Load as you go.** Keep the box filling throughout the project, not all at the end.
- **Call for pickup when done,** which can free your driveway (though it does not refund unused days).
- **Extend ahead** if the job runs long, before the period ends, rather than after.
- **Consider a swap** for a phased job, so the box matches the debris flow.

The period is a resource you have paid for, so timing the box to the actual work, rather than having it arrive early and sit, is how you make the most of it.
