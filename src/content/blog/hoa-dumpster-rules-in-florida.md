# HOA Dumpster Rules in Florida

In Florida's many HOA and deed-restricted communities, the association's rules add a layer on top of city and county rules. This pillar post covers HOA dumpster rules.

## The HOA Layer

Many Southwest Florida communities are deed-restricted with an HOA, and the association can set its own rules on dumpster rental, on top of the city or county rules:

- **Placement:** where the box may sit (often driveway only, not the street or common areas).
- **Duration:** how long it may stay.
- **Screening:** whether it must be screened from view.
- **Delivery hours:** when delivery and pickup may happen.
- **Notice or approval:** some require advance notice or approval.

So in an HOA community, check the association's rules before booking, in addition to the city/county permit question. A box on your own driveway still generally avoids a government permit, but the HOA rules apply regardless. Confirm placement and duration with the HOA, and clear delivery timing with the hauler.
