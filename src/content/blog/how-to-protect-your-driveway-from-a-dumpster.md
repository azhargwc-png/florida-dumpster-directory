# How to Protect Your Driveway From a Dumpster

A loaded dumpster is heavy, and the delivery truck adds point loads, so protecting the driveway, especially pavers, matters. This post covers how.

## Protecting the Surface

A loaded roll-off can weigh several tons, concentrated on its contact points, and the delivery truck's wheels add more. On a paver, stamped-concrete or sealed driveway, that can crack, crush or stain the surface. To protect it:

- **Lay boards** (two-by-tens or sheets of plywood) under where the box's rails will rest, to spread the load.
- **Lay boards along the truck's path** if the driveway is delicate, since the truck is heaviest.
- **Ask the hauler to set the box on your boards,** and confirm they will.
- **Choose a firm, level spot** so the box sits evenly.

Pavers are the main concern in Southwest Florida, where they are common and costly to repair; boards under the contact points prevent individual pavers from cracking or shifting. Plain concrete and asphalt are more forgiving but can still stain or mark, so boards are cheap insurance.
