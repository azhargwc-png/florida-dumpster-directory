# Protecting a Sealed or Stamped Concrete Driveway

Sealed and stamped concrete driveways have finishes that a dumpster can stain or mar, so they need protection beyond what plain concrete needs. This post covers it.

## Protecting the Finish

Sealed and stamped (decorative) concrete have surface finishes, a sealer coat, a stamped texture and color, that are more vulnerable than bare concrete to:

- **Staining** from the box's metal contact points or any residue.
- **Scuffing or scratching** the sealer.
- **Cracking or chipping** the decorative surface under point loads.

To protect them: lay boards (planks or plywood) under the box's rails and along the truck's path, as with pavers, to spread the load and keep metal off the finish. The boards both distribute the weight and act as a barrier against staining and scuffing. Choose a firm spot, and ask the hauler to set the box on your boards. The decorative or sealed finish is costly to restore, so the board protection, cheap and simple, is well worth it. Have the boards down before delivery.
