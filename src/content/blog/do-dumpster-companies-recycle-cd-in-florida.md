# Do Dumpster Companies Recycle C&D in Florida?

Many dumpster companies recycle construction and demolition (C and D) debris, and in Lee County it is required. This pillar post covers C and D recycling.

## C and D Recycling in Florida

Construction and demolition debris, concrete, wood, metal, drywall, roofing, can often be recycled rather than landfilled, and whether it is depends on the hauler, the facility, and the county:

- **Lee County requires it.** A mandatory C and D recycling ordinance means construction debris is routed to recycling-capable facilities, and haulers serving Lee County handle this.
- **Clean loads recycle best.** Clean concrete becomes aggregate, clean metal and wood are diverted; mixed debris is harder to recycle.
- **Other counties vary.** Recycling happens where the facility and hauler support it, even without a mandate.

So C and D is often recycled, especially in Lee County and from clean loads. Keeping heavy material in clean, separate loads supports it. Describe your debris to the hauler, who routes it. See the Lee County construction debris recycling post.
