# How Dumpster Weight Overage Works

A weight overage is the per-ton charge when your load exceeds its included weight allowance. Knowing how it is calculated makes it easy to avoid. This post explains it.

## How Overage Is Calculated

Your rental includes a weight allowance (a number of tons). At pickup, the loaded box is weighed at the disposal facility. If the load exceeds the allowance, the excess is billed per ton, the overage rate, on top of the base price. For example, an allowance exceeded by a ton is billed one ton at the overage rate (the actual rate varies by hauler, so ask).

Why it exists: the disposal facility charges the hauler by weight, so a heavier load costs more to dispose of. To avoid an overage:

- **Keep heavy debris in a small box filled partway,** to the allowance.
- **Describe heavy material up front** so the allowance fits the job.
- **Do not top a box off past the hauler's fill guidance.**

Light, bulky debris rarely triggers an overage, so the risk is mainly with dense material.
