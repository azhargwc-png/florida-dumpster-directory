# 30-Yard vs 40-Yard Dumpster

The 30-yard and 40-yard are the two largest common roll-offs, used for big renovation, construction and commercial jobs. The 40-yard's extra-tall walls make it the choice only for very high volumes of light, bulky debris.

## The 30-Yard

Best for a whole-home gut, large demolition or rebuild. Typical jobs: a full renovation, a large estate cleanout, new construction debris, a major demolition.

## The 40-Yard

Best for large commercial and construction jobs with light, bulky debris. Typical jobs: a large commercial cleanout, major new construction, a whole-building demolition, high volumes of packaging or lightweight material.

## Which to Choose

Choose the 30-yard for most big residential jobs, and the 40-yard mainly for commercial and large construction work with high volumes of light, bulky debris. The 40-yard's very tall walls make loading heavier debris impractical and risk overloading it, so it is reserved for genuinely high-volume, lightweight loads. For a home project, the 30-yard is almost always the larger size you need.

Describe your project to the hauler, the type of job, the rooms or square footage, and the kind of debris, and they will confirm the right size. The [size calculator](/tools/dumpster-size-calculator/) is a good starting point.
