# How to Plan a Cleanout With a Dumpster

Any cleanout goes faster with a plan. This post is the general method for planning a cleanout around a dumpster, applicable to a garage, estate, move or downsizing.

## The Cleanout Plan

A cleanout goes smoothly with a simple sequence:

**1. Sort before the box arrives.** Separate keep, give to family, sell, donate, hazardous (to the county facility), and trash. Having this done means the box fills with trash, not indecision.
**2. Size the box.** Most cleanout debris is light and bulky, so size by volume: 10-15 yard for a room or garage, 20 for a whole house, 30 for a large or packed home.
**3. Time the delivery** to when sorting is done or scheduled, so the box is not idle on the clock.
**4. Load efficiently.** Big flat items first, fill gaps, break down bulky items, keep the load level with the fill line.
**5. Route the exceptions.** Hazardous materials, electronics, tires and appliances to the county facility, not the box.

Describe the cleanout to the hauler, who sizes the box and advises on swaps for a big job.
