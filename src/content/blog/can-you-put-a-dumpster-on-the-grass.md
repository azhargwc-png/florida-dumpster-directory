# Can You Put a Dumpster on the Grass?

You can sometimes place a dumpster on grass, but soft ground risks ruts, sinking and a stuck truck, especially in Florida's wet season. This post covers the risks and how to do it safely.

## Grass and Soft Ground

Placing a dumpster on grass is possible but carries risks:

- **The loaded box and the truck are heavy,** so they can sink into soft ground, rut the lawn, and in the worst case leave the truck stuck.
- **Florida's wet season** saturates the ground, making this worse; sandy soil can also give way.
- **Lawn damage** from the box's contact points and the truck's wheels is likely.

To do it more safely: choose the firmest, most level grass area, lay plywood under the box's rails and ideally along the truck's path to spread the load, keep the box on the smaller side, and avoid it after heavy rain. Even so, a firm driveway is almost always the better spot. If you must use grass, describe the ground to the hauler, who will judge whether the truck can place and retrieve the box safely.
