# What Size Dumpster for a Construction Project

Choosing a dumpster size for a construction project comes down to the debris type and volume. Construction sizing depends on scope: a 20-yard for a moderate job, a 30- or 40-yard for a large build. In Lee County, remember the mandatory C and D recycling requirement.

## Sizing A construction project

A moderate construction project fits a 20-yard box; a large build or whole-structure job may need a 30- or 40-yard, or scheduled swaps. Construction debris is a mix, so keep heavy material (concrete, masonry) in a separate small box filled partway. In Lee County, the mandatory construction-and-demolition recycling requirement applies, and a hauler routes the load accordingly. Describe the project scope and debris to the hauler.

## Weight vs Volume

The reason sizing is not just about volume is that a dumpster also has a weight limit. Light, bulky debris fills the volume first; heavy material (concrete, tile, dirt, stone) reaches the weight limit first and goes in a smaller box filled partway. Matching the box to both factors is what avoids an overage charge or a second haul. See the [construction dumpster page](/construction-dumpster-rental/).

## Confirm With the Hauler

The surest way to size a construction project is to describe it to the hauler: the scope, the rooms or square footage, and the kind of debris. The [size calculator](/tools/dumpster-size-calculator/) gives a starting estimate, and the hauler confirms the box and weight allowance from there, so you neither run out of room nor pay for space you do not use.
