# Estero Dumpster Permit and Placement Rules

Renting a dumpster in Estero, FL means knowing who governs placement, where a box can sit, and where your debris goes. This guide covers the permit question, placement, and the local Lee County facilities.

## Who Governs Placement in Estero

Estero is an incorporated village in Lee County but relies on Lee County Solid Waste for collection, so a box on a public right-of-way follows the applicable right-of-way rules, and a box on your own driveway generally needs no permit. Gated and deed-restricted communities are common and may add their own rules.

## Placement and Local Factors

Estero has many gated and master-planned communities, which often set their own rules on dumpster placement, duration and screening, so check your HOA before booking.

**On your own driveway:** a box entirely on your private driveway usually needs no permit, which is the simplest option. Protect a paver or finished driveway with boards under the roll-off's contact points.

**On the street or right-of-way:** a box there follows the rules of whoever owns the road, so confirm the current requirement before placing one.

## Where Your Debris Goes

Estero's garbage, recycling and yard waste are handled by Lee County Solid Waste, and hazardous waste goes free to the Lee County Topaz Court facility at 6441 Topaz Court, Fort Myers. The county's mandatory C and D recycling applies. Keep hazardous waste, electronics and appliances out of the dumpster.

It depends on the job and the debris weight. A cleanout or shingle re-roof usually fits a 20-yard box, a bathroom remodel a 15-yard, and a whole-home renovation a 20- or 30-yard. Heavy debris like concrete or tile goes in a smaller 10-yard box filled partway, since weight hits the limit before the box looks full.
