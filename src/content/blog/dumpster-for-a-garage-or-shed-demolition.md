# Dumpster for a Garage or Shed Demolition

Demolishing a garage or shed produces a manageable burst of mixed debris plus, often, a concrete slab. This post covers sizing it.

## Sizing a Garage or Shed Teardown

A detached garage or shed demolition generates framing lumber, roofing, siding, doors and any interior debris, plus the concrete slab if you remove it. To handle it:

- **General structure debris** (wood, roofing, siding) is bulky but light-to-moderate, so a 20-yard often holds a single-car garage or large shed; a small shed may fit a 15-yard.
- **The concrete slab** is heavy and goes in a separate small 10-yard box filled partway if you break it up and remove it, or stays if you leave it.
- **Roofing** adds weight, especially if tile.

So a garage demolition with slab removal is often a two-box job: a larger box for the structure and a small box for the slab. Describe the structure, whether the slab is coming out, and the roof type to the hauler. See the [demolition dumpster page](/demolition-dumpster-rental/).
