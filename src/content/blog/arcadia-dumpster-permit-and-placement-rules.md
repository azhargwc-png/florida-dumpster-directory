# Arcadia Dumpster Permit and Placement Rules

Renting a dumpster in Arcadia, FL means knowing who governs placement, where a box can sit, and where your debris goes. This guide covers the permit question, placement, and the local DeSoto County facilities.

## Who Governs Placement in Arcadia

Arcadia is an incorporated city and the DeSoto County seat, so the City of Arcadia governs its streets (with its own Solid Waste Division), while DeSoto County governs the unincorporated areas. A box on your own property generally needs no permit; a street box follows the applicable rules.

## Placement and Local Factors

Arcadia is a historic, rural agricultural city, so dumpster jobs often involve older-home renovation, agricultural and land-clearing work, and mobile-home cleanouts. Many lots have room for private placement; confirm access on a rural drive.

**On your own driveway:** a box entirely on your private driveway usually needs no permit, which is the simplest option. Protect a paver or finished driveway with boards under the roll-off's contact points.

**On the street or right-of-way:** a box there follows the rules of whoever owns the road, so confirm the current requirement before placing one.

## Where Your Debris Goes

Arcadia uses the DeSoto County Landfill at 3268 SW Dishong Avenue (loads accepted Monday through Saturday, 7:30 to 5:00) and the permitted Class III site, DeSoto Recycling and Disposal, at 13620 SE HWY-70 for construction debris and yard waste. Hazardous waste is handled carefully in this rural county; check with DeSoto County for current options. Keep hazardous waste and appliances out of the dumpster.
