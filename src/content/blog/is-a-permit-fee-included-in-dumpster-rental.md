# Is a Permit Fee Included in Dumpster Rental?

A permit for placing a dumpster on a street or right-of-way is usually separate from the rental price. Whether you need one depends on where the box sits. This post covers when a permit applies and who handles it.

## When a Permit Applies and Who Pays

A permit is generally needed only when the box sits on a public street or right-of-way, not on your own driveway. In most Southwest Florida communities, the swale fronting your lot is public right-of-way, so a box there needs a permit too. The permit is issued by the city or county that owns the road, and its cost is a separate charge from the rental, so it is usually not included in the hauler's base price. Who arranges it varies: some haulers will pull the permit for you (and pass through the cost), while others ask you to obtain it. The simplest way to avoid the permit entirely is to place the box on your own driveway, where none is generally needed.
