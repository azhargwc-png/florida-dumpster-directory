# What Size Dumpster for Concrete

Choosing a dumpster size for concrete comes down to the debris type and volume. Concrete needs a 10-yard box filled only partway, because it reaches the weight limit long before the box looks full. A larger box of concrete would be too heavy to haul safely.

## Sizing Concrete

Concrete goes in a 10-yard box filled only partway. Because concrete is dense, the weight limit is reached well before the box fills, so a 10-yard is the standard and you fill to the weight allowance, not the rim. Keep it a clean concrete load (no wood or trash) so it can be recycled into aggregate. For a large slab or driveway, you may need more than one haul.

## Weight vs Volume

The reason sizing is not just about volume is that a dumpster also has a weight limit. Light, bulky debris fills the volume first; heavy material (concrete, tile, dirt, stone) reaches the weight limit first and goes in a smaller box filled partway. Matching the box to both factors is what avoids an overage charge or a second haul. See the [concrete dumpster page](/concrete-dumpster-rental/).

## Confirm With the Hauler

The surest way to size concrete is to describe it to the hauler: the scope, the rooms or square footage, and the kind of debris. The [size calculator](/tools/dumpster-size-calculator/) gives a starting estimate, and the hauler confirms the box and weight allowance from there, so you neither run out of room nor pay for space you do not use.
