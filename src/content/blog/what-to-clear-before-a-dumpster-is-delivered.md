# What to Clear Before a Dumpster Is Delivered

Clearing the spot and approach before delivery lets the driver place the box exactly where you need it and avoids a wasted-trip fee. This post is the pre-delivery clearing checklist.

## The Clearing Checklist

Before the truck arrives, clear and prepare:

- **The placement spot:** move vehicles, trailers, bins and anything on the spot where the box will sit.
- **The approach:** clear the driveway or path the truck will back in along.
- **Overhead:** check for and clear low branches, and note any wires or low rooflines the bed could hit.
- **Gates and access:** open any gate, and unlock access the truck needs.
- **Fragile items:** move anything near the spot that could be damaged.
- **Surface protection:** lay boards if it is a paver or finished driveway.
- **Marking:** mark the exact spot and orientation you want.

With the spot and approach clear, delivery takes minutes and the box lands where you need it. An uncleared spot can delay delivery or turn it into a wasted trip with a fee, so do this before the delivery window.
