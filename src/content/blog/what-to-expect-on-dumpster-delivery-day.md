# What to Expect on Dumpster Delivery Day

On delivery day, a roll-off truck brings the box and sets it on your agreed spot. Knowing what to expect and how to prepare makes it quick and protects your property.

## How Delivery Works

A roll-off truck arrives and positions to place the box on the spot you agreed. The driver tilts the bed and rolls the box off (that is the 'roll-off' name), so they need a clear, straight approach and overhead clearance (no low branches or wires). To prepare:

- **Clear the spot** of vehicles, obstacles and anything overhead.
- **Lay boards** under where the box will rest if it is a paver or finished driveway.
- **Mark the exact placement** you want, so the driver sets it where you need it (for example, with the door-end facing your work).
- **Be available if access is needed,** such as opening a gate.

Delivery itself is quick once the spot is ready. If the truck cannot place the box (blocked spot, no clearance), it may be a wasted trip with a fee, so prepare the spot beforehand.
