# How to Protect Pavers Under a Dumpster

Paver driveways are common in Southwest Florida and vulnerable to a dumpster's concentrated weight. Boards under the contact points protect them. This post covers how.

## Protecting Pavers

Pavers are set in sand and individually vulnerable to point loads, so a loaded dumpster's rails, and the delivery truck's wheels, can crack pavers or push them out of alignment. To protect them:

- **Lay sturdy boards** (two-by-ten planks or 3/4-inch plywood) under where the box's rails will rest, spreading the load across many pavers.
- **Lay boards along the truck's path** too, since the truck is the heaviest part.
- **Ask the hauler to set the box on your boards,** and confirm they will.
- **Choose a firm, well-set area** of the driveway, avoiding any pavers already loose.

The boards spread the concentrated weight so no individual paver takes the full load. This is cheap insurance against a costly paver repair, and it is the main reason pavers need more care than plain concrete. Have the boards down before delivery.
