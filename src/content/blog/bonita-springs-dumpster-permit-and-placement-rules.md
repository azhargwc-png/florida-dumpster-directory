# Bonita Springs Dumpster Permit and Placement Rules

Renting a dumpster in Bonita Springs, FL means knowing who governs placement, where a box can sit, and where your debris goes. This guide covers the permit question, placement, and the local Lee County facilities.

## Who Governs Placement in Bonita Springs

Bonita Springs is an incorporated city in south Lee County, so the City of Bonita Springs governs its streets and rights-of-way. A box on a city street or right-of-way follows city rules; a box on your own driveway generally needs none.

## Placement and Local Factors

Bonita Springs has many gated and deed-restricted communities, which may add their own rules on dumpster placement and duration, so check your HOA before booking.

**On your own driveway:** a box entirely on your private driveway usually needs no permit, which is the simplest option. Protect a paver or finished driveway with boards under the roll-off's contact points.

**On the street or right-of-way:** a box there follows the rules of whoever owns the road, so confirm the current requirement before placing one.

## Where Your Debris Goes

Bonita Springs uses the Lee County Topaz Court facility at 6441 Topaz Court, Fort Myers for household hazardous waste and electronics, free for residents. The county's mandatory C and D recycling applies. Keep hazardous waste, electronics and appliances out of the dumpster.

**Do Bonita Springs communities have extra dumpster rules?**
They can. Bonita Springs has many gated and deed-restricted communities, which may set their own rules on dumpster placement, duration, screening and delivery hours on top of the city rules. Check your HOA or community association before booking, confirm where the box can sit and gate access, and clear delivery timing with the hauler. The City of Bonita Springs rules for streets and the Lee County hazardous-waste drop-off at Topaz Court still apply in addition.

**What size dumpster do I need in Bonita Springs?**
It depends on the job and the debris weight. A cleanout or shingle re-roof usually fits a 20-yard box, a bathroom remodel a 15-yard, and a whole-home renovation a 20- or 30-yard. Heavy debris like concrete or tile goes in a smaller 10-yard box filled partway, since weight hits the limit before the box looks full. Confirm the size by weight with a dumpster company serving Bonita Springs, and keep hazardous waste, electronics and appliances out of the box.
