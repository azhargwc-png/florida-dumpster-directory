# 20-Yard vs 30-Yard Dumpster

The 20-yard and 30-yard have the same footprint; the 30-yard just has taller walls and holds half again as much. The choice is about how much volume a big job will produce, since both are for light-to-moderate debris, not heavy material.

## The 20-Yard

Best for most renovation and cleanout jobs. Typical jobs: a whole-house cleanout, a shingle re-roof, a multi-room renovation.

## The 30-Yard

Best for a whole-home gut or large-volume job. Typical jobs: a full renovation or gut, a large estate cleanout, new construction debris, a major demolition or storm rebuild.

## Which to Choose

Choose the 20-yard for most renovation and cleanout work, and the 30-yard when the job is a whole-home gut, a large demolition, or a rebuild that produces high volume. Because the 30-yard has taller walls, it is for bulky construction debris; heavy material still goes in a small 10-yard filled partway regardless of the big box you choose for the light debris.

Describe your project to the hauler, the type of job, the rooms or square footage, and the kind of debris, and they will confirm the right size. The [size calculator](/tools/dumpster-size-calculator/) is a good starting point.
