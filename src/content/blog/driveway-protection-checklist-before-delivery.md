# Driveway Protection Checklist Before Delivery

A few minutes before delivery protects your driveway from the box and truck. This post is the pre-delivery driveway-protection checklist.

## The Checklist

Before the dumpster arrives:

- **Choose a firm, level spot** on the driveway for the box.
- **Lay boards** (two-by-ten planks or 3/4-inch plywood) where the box's rails will rest.
- **Lay boards along the truck's path** on a delicate surface (pavers, stamped/sealed concrete, asphalt).
- **Clear the spot and approach** of vehicles and obstacles.
- **Check overhead** for low branches or wires over the spot and approach.
- **Note delicate areas** (loose pavers, sealed sections) to point out or avoid.
- **Mark the placement** and ask the hauler to set the box on your boards.
- **Time it for cooler hours** if the driveway is asphalt.

With this done, the box lands on protection and the truck rolls over boards where it matters, so the driveway comes through the rental undamaged. Have everything ready before the delivery window.
