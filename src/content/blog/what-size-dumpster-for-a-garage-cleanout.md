# What Size Dumpster for a Garage Cleanout

Choosing a dumpster size for a garage cleanout comes down to the debris type and volume. A garage cleanout usually fits a 10- or 15-yard box, depending on how packed the garage is. The debris is mostly light and bulky, so volume governs.

## Sizing A garage cleanout

A typical garage cleanout fits a 10- or 15-yard box. Garage debris is mostly light and bulky, boxes, old furniture, tools, general junk, so volume governs rather than weight. A packed two- or three-car garage, or one with heavy items, may warrant a 20-yard. Set aside hazardous items (paint, oil, chemicals, propane) for your county facility.

## Weight vs Volume

The reason sizing is not just about volume is that a dumpster also has a weight limit. Light, bulky debris fills the volume first; heavy material (concrete, tile, dirt, stone) reaches the weight limit first and goes in a smaller box filled partway. Matching the box to both factors is what avoids an overage charge or a second haul. See the [15-yard dumpster page](/15-yard-dumpster-rental/).

## Confirm With the Hauler

The surest way to size a garage cleanout is to describe it to the hauler: the scope, the rooms or square footage, and the kind of debris. The [size calculator](/tools/dumpster-size-calculator/) gives a starting estimate, and the hauler confirms the box and weight allowance from there, so you neither run out of room nor pay for space you do not use.
