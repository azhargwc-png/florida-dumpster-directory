# Private Dumpster for Storm Rebuild Work

Rebuild and repair debris from fixing storm damage is construction debris, not storm debris, so a private dumpster is the right tool. This post explains the distinction and how to rent for rebuild work.

## Rebuild Debris Is Construction Debris

After the initial storm cleanup, the rebuild phase, tearing out damaged drywall, flooring, cabinetry and roofing, and replacing them, generates construction and demolition debris. That is distinct from the storm debris the county collects at the right-of-way, and it is what a private dumpster is for. Rent a roll-off sized to the rebuild scope, keep the debris clean of hazardous items and appliances (which go to the county facility), and schedule the box to your repair timeline. In Lee County, the mandatory C and D recycling requirement applies to rebuild debris, so the hauler routes it accordingly. A private dumpster for rebuild work never competes with the county's curbside storm program.
