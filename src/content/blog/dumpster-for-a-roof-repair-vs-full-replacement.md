# Dumpster for a Roof Repair vs Full Replacement

A full re-roof needs a dumpster; a small repair often does not. This post covers the line between them and how to size each.

## Repair vs Replacement

The two differ in debris volume:

- **A small roof repair** (patching a section, replacing a few shingles or tiles after a leak or minor storm damage) produces little debris, often a small amount that regular disposal or a contractor's truck handles. A dumpster may be more than you need.
- **A full roof replacement** (tearing off the whole roof) produces a full load, a 20-yard for shingle or a 10-yard filled partway for tile, so a dumpster is the right tool.

So size by the scope: a full tear-off needs a roofing dumpster sized to the roof type; a spot repair usually does not. A larger repair (a significant section) falls in between and may warrant a small box. Describe the roof work to the hauler, who will tell you whether a dumpster fits the job.
