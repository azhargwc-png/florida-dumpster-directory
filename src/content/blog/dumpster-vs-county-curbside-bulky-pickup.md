# Dumpster vs County Curbside Bulky Pickup

For getting rid of bulky items, you can use a county curbside bulky pickup or rent a dumpster. This pillar comparison covers which fits.

## Bulky Pickup vs Dumpster

**County curbside bulky pickup** collects a limited number of large items (furniture, appliances per the rules) from the curb on a schedule, within county limits. It is free or low-cost but limited in volume and timing, and governed by what the county accepts.

**A rented dumpster** handles a large volume on your schedule, for a cleanout or renovation that exceeds bulky-pickup limits.

So use county bulky pickup for a few large items within the limits, and a dumpster for a volume cleanout or project. Check your county's bulky-pickup rules (item limits, scheduling, what is accepted). Describe a larger job to the hauler for a dumpster size.
