# Dumpster on Pavers vs Asphalt vs Concrete

The three common driveway surfaces, pavers, asphalt and concrete, react differently to a dumpster's weight. This post compares them and the protection each needs.

## Surface by Surface

**Pavers:** individual units set in sand, so a point load can crack one or push it out of alignment. Highest care; boards essential under the box and truck path. Repairs costly.

**Asphalt:** a flexible surface that can dent or depress under concentrated weight, and the risk rises in Florida heat when asphalt softens. Boards advised; deliver in the cooler part of the day if possible.

**Concrete:** a rigid continuous slab that distributes load best, so lowest risk, though extreme point loads can crack it and the surface can mark or stain (more so if stamped or sealed). Boards are cheap insurance.

Across all three, boards under the box's rails and the truck's path spread the load and prevent most damage. The difference is degree: pavers need the most care, asphalt needs heat-aware timing, and concrete is most forgiving but still worth protecting. Match the protection to your surface.
