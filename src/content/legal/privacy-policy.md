# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 | Site owner: Azhar Uddin Khan, November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Florida Dumpster Directory ("we," "us," or "the site") handles information when you use floridadumpsterdirectory.com. By using the site, you agree to the practices described here.

## Who We Are

Florida Dumpster Directory is a free directory and referral resource that helps homeowners and contractors in Southwest Florida connect with local dumpster rental companies. We are not a dumpster rental company. Work is performed by independent professionals.

## Information We Collect

**Information you provide.** When you call the phone number on this site to connect you with a local provider, the information you share on that call, such as your name, location, and project details, is used to connect you with a local dumpster rental company that serves your area.

**Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, we may collect standard technical information through your browser and standard web analytics, such as your approximate location, device type, browser, pages viewed, and how you arrived at the site. This helps us understand how the site is used and improve it.

**Cookies and analytics.** We may use cookies and third-party analytics tools to measure site traffic and performance. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not work as well if you do.

## How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Connect you with a local dumpster rental provider when you call
- Operate, maintain and improve the site
- Understand how visitors use the site
- Respond to questions or requests

We do not sell your personal information.

## Sharing Information

When you call to be connected with a local provider, relevant details you share are passed to a local dumpster rental company so they can assist you. We may also share information with service providers who help us operate the site, such as analytics and hosting providers, and we may disclose information where required by law.

## Third-Party Sites

The companies listed in this directory are independent businesses, and any site or service you reach through them is governed by that company's own privacy practices, not this policy. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of independent providers or other third parties.

## Your Choices

You can control cookies through your browser, and you can choose not to call or share information with us. If you have a question about information you shared on a call, contact us through the phone number on the site.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent version. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

## Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, reach us through the [Contact](/contact/) page.
