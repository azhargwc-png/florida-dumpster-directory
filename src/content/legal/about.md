# About Florida Dumpster Directory

Florida Dumpster Directory is a free resource that helps homeowners and contractors across Southwest Florida find and reach local dumpster rental companies. We cover 23 cities in six counties, Lee, Collier, Charlotte, Sarasota, DeSoto and Hendry, from Sarasota and Venice in the north down through Fort Myers, Cape Coral and Naples to Marco Island and Immokalee in the south.

## What We Do

We make it simpler to rent a roll-off dumpster in Southwest Florida. Each city page lists local dumpster companies serving that area along with their Google review counts, explains the roll-off sizes that fit common local projects, and covers the placement and permit rules that apply in that city and county. Our guides and tools help you plan the job before you call, so you know what size you need and what to ask.

When you are ready, a single call connects you with a local dumpster company that serves your city.

## How We Help You

Southwest Florida is not one market but six counties, each with its own permit offices, landfills and debris rules. A roll-off that is permit-free on your own driveway can need paperwork a few miles away across a county line, and the region's heavy tile roofs and canal lots add wrinkles that generic national sites miss. We organize everything by city and county so you get the information that actually applies to your property, not a one-size-fits-all answer.

## What We Are Not

We are not a dumpster rental company, and we do not own trucks or containers. We are a directory and referral resource that connects you with independent local companies. Work is performed by independent professionals, and it is the homeowner's responsibility to confirm that any company they hire carries the necessary license and insurance for the work.
